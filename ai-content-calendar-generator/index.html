AI Content Calendar Generator

This tool builds a dated content calendar from a start date you pick, with your niche, platforms, posts per week and number of weeks. It spreads the posts evenly across each week and shows a table of dates, platforms, ideas and formats you can copy or download.

The ideas are starting points from a built-in bank, not a strategy. Change them to fit your audience, and check dates against your own holidays and events. This tool works fully in your browser and calls no AI service, API, or backend.

How to use this generator

Enter your niche, tick the platforms you post on, pick a start date, and choose how many posts per week and how many weeks you want to plan. Press the button and the tool builds a dated table with one row per post: the date, the platform, a content idea, and a format.

Copy the table into your planner or download it as a text file. Then rewrite each idea in your own words and adjust any date that clashes with something you already have planned.

How the dates are worked out

Posts are spread evenly across each week. With three posts a week, they land on the first, third, and fifth days of the week; with two, on the first and fourth days; with one, on the first day. The pattern repeats each week from your start date, so the calendar stays regular without bunching posts together.

Platforms rotate in the order you ticked them, so each platform gets a fair share of the posts. Formats rotate too, through posts, short videos, stories, carousels, articles, and live sessions, so the plan does not repeat the same format every time.

Making the ideas your own

The idea bank covers common formats that work in most niches: tips, behind-the-scenes looks, mistakes to avoid, questions answered, and short stories. Treat each idea as a title to rewrite, not a finished post. Add your own examples, your own photos, and the details only you know.

Batch similar work where you can. Writing all the tips in one sitting, or filming several short videos on the same day, is usually faster than switching between formats. Keep the calendar visible while you work so nothing slips.

Honest limits

This tool plans dates and ideas. It cannot post for you, check whether an idea suits your audience, or know about events and holidays in your calendar. Check the dates before you commit to them, especially around public holidays.

A calendar is only useful if you follow it loosely. If a better idea comes up on the day, use it. The plan is a safety net for busy weeks, not a contract.

Questions people ask

How are the post dates chosen?

Posts are spread evenly across each week from your start date. Three posts a week land on the first, third, and fifth days; two land on the first and fourth days. The pattern repeats every week.

Can I change the ideas?

Yes. The ideas are starting points from a built-in bank. Rewrite them in your own words, swap any that do not fit, and add your own examples before you post.

What if I post on more platforms?

Tick the platforms you use and the calendar rotates between them. If you use a platform that is not listed, treat one of the columns as a stand-in and rename it in your own planner.

Does this tool post for me?

No. It plans the calendar only. You write the posts and publish them yourself, on whatever schedule you choose.
