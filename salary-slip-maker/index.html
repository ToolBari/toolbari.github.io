What goes on a salary slip

A salary slip names the employer and the employee, states the pay month, and lists earnings and deductions with their totals. The net pay at the bottom is what the employee takes home for that month.

This maker works out both totals and the net pay for you, and prints the working on the slip so the figures can be checked at a glance. Earnings and deductions are kept in separate lists, each with its own total.

Checking the figures

Net pay is total earnings minus total deductions. If the deductions are larger than the earnings, the net pay will be negative, which usually means a figure was typed wrong, so check the lines before you print.

All figures on the slip are the numbers you type. This maker never suggests salary amounts or deduction rates, and it does not check your figures against any payroll rules.

Keeping slips

Give the employee a copy of each slip and keep one for your own records, in order by month. A slip is a record of what was paid and what was taken off, so it is worth keeping even after the pay has gone through.

If a slip has a mistake, make a corrected slip for the same month and keep both, so the record shows what changed. Do not throw away the old slip, because the two together explain the correction.

Questions people ask

How is net pay worked out?

Net pay is total earnings minus total deductions. Both totals are shown on the slip with the working beside them.

Can I add more earnings or deductions?

Yes. Use the Add buttons under each list to add more lines, and Remove on any line to take it out.

What should I put as the pay month?

Use the month the slip covers, for example the month the salary was paid for. The slip shows it exactly as you pick it.

How do I print or keep the slip?

Use the Print button to open the print dialog with just the slip, or download the slip as a file to keep or send.
