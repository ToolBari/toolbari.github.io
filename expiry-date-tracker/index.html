Expiry Date Tracker

Keep track of expiry dates for food, medicine and household items in one list. Add an item with its expiry date and see at a glance how many days are left, what has expired, and what needs attention soon.

Your list is saved only in this browser. Clearing browser data removes it, and the dates you enter are only as accurate as the labels you read, so always check the item itself before use.

How the warnings work

Every item is compared with today's date. An item whose date has passed is marked expired, with how many days ago it expired. An item expiring within the next seven days, including today, is marked expiring soon. Everything else simply shows how many days are left.

The list is always sorted with the earliest expiry date first, so the items that need your attention are at the top. Adding a new item places it in the right spot automatically.

Tips for using the tracker

Add items when you buy them or when you first open them, using the date printed on the label. A quick weekly look at the top of the list is enough to catch things before they expire. For items you use every day, such as food in the fridge, the expiring-soon warning is the one to watch.

The tracker is a memory aid, not a safety test. If an item looks or smells wrong, do not use it even if its date has not passed, and follow the storage instructions on the label.

What to track

Food and drink are the obvious starting point, but the tracker works for anything with a date on it. Medicine, vitamins and first-aid supplies all expire, and an out-of-date packet is easy to miss in a crowded cupboard. Household items such as sunscreen, batteries and cleaning products also carry expiry dates worth tracking.

Documents work well too: passports, driving licences, warranties and subscriptions all have end dates, and the expiring-soon warning gives you time to renew instead of discovering the problem at the last minute.

Questions people ask

Where is my list saved?

Your list is saved in your own browser using local storage. It is never uploaded anywhere, and it stays on this device until you clear it or clear your browser data.

What counts as expiring soon?

An item counts as expiring soon when its expiry date falls within the next seven days, including today. Items with more than seven days left simply show the number of days remaining.

Can I edit an item?

To change an item, delete it with its Delete button and add it again with the corrected name or date. The list is re-sorted automatically.

Does it send reminders?

No. The tracker shows your list and warnings when you open the page, but it does not send notifications or reminders on its own.
