Voice Changer

Have fun changing how your voice sounds, right in your browser. Record with your microphone or upload a voice clip, pick an effect, listen to the result, then export the changed recording.

Record with your microphone or upload a clip to begin.

Effects are for fun only — never use a changed voice to pretend to be someone else, and only record people who know they are being recorded.

How each effect works

High pitch plays your voice faster, which raises the pitch and makes it sound small and squeaky. Low pitch plays it slower, which drops the pitch and makes it sound deep and slow. The words stay the same — only the speed and pitch change.

Echo repeats your voice after a short delay, like shouting in a large empty room. Robot flattens the sound wave into a hard on-off shape, which gives the voice a metallic, machine-like edge. Pick an effect, press play, and try another if you want a different sound.

Ideas for using it

Use the voice changer for party games — guess who is speaking behind the robot voice — or for storytelling, giving each character a different voice. It is also fun for short voice greetings and silly messages to friends.

Keep it kind and honest. A changed voice is a toy, not a disguise: never use it to pretend to be someone else, and only record people who know they are being recorded and are happy with it.

Recording a clean clip

Effects sound best on a clear recording. Hold the phone or microphone about a hand's width from your mouth, speak at a normal volume, and record somewhere quiet — a room with soft furnishings beats a bare room with an echo.

Keep clips short while you experiment: a few seconds is enough to hear what each effect does. Once you like an effect, record the full clip and export it.

Questions people ask

Does this tool upload my recording?

No. Recording, effects and export all happen in your browser.

Do I need a microphone?

Only for recording. You can also upload an existing voice clip instead.

Which effects are included?

High pitch, low pitch, echo and robot, plus the original voice with no effect.

What does the export contain?

The export records your clip played through the selected effect, so the downloaded file sounds like what you heard.
