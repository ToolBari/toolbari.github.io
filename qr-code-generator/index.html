Step by step

  1. Open the QR Code Generator tool.
  2. Choose the type: Link / Text, Wi-Fi or Phone number.
  3. Fill in the details — the link, or your Wi-Fi name (SSID) and password.
  4. Pick a size (256px, 512px, or 1024px for print), press Create QR Code, then Download PNG.

When this is useful

  • Shops and restaurants: let customers scan for a menu, a page or payment information.
  • Homes and offices: a Wi-Fi QR on the wall lets guests join without asking for the password.
  • Visiting cards, posters and event banners that point to a link or phone number.

Questions people ask

Does a QR code from this generator expire?

No. The information is encoded directly into the image, so it keeps working as long as the content stays valid. One exception: a Wi-Fi QR stops working if you change the Wi-Fi password — just make a new one.

Can I print the QR code?

Yes — choose the 1024px size before creating it; that is sharp enough for posters, cards and shop displays.

Is my Wi-Fi password shared with ToolBari?

The generator works in your browser and simply encodes what you type into the QR image. Treat the printed QR like the password itself — share it only with people you actually want on your network.

Is the QR Code Generator free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up and no watermark on the downloaded PNG.
