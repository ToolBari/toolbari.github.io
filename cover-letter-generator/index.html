Ready for the job-circular season

Job circulars in Bangladesh come in waves, and the December–January wave is one of the biggest. A CV alone is often not enough — many circulars ask for a short cover letter with the application. Build the CV, generate the letter, and size the photo before the wave starts, so each application takes minutes instead of an evening.

Questions people ask

Is the Cover Letter Generator free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, and it runs in your browser on any phone or computer.

What do I need to type into the Cover Letter Generator?

Four things: your name, the job title, the company name, and one key skill. The generator turns them into a ready letter template.

How long should a cover letter be?

One page is the standard. Say which job you want, name one skill that fits it, and ask for the interview — then stop.

Do I need a different cover letter for each job?

Yes. Change the job title, the company name and the key skill for every application, so each letter matches the job it goes with.
