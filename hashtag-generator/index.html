Questions people ask

How many hashtags should I use on a post?

Around 15–25 is enough for most posts. Use a mix of broad tags that many people follow and smaller topic tags that match your exact post — a wall of unrelated tags does not help.

Can I use these hashtags on Facebook, YouTube and TikTok?

Yes. The same set can be pasted into a Facebook post, a YouTube title or description, a TikTok caption or an Instagram Reel.

Is my topic sent to a server?

No. This quick generator runs in your browser; the category and topic you type are not uploaded by this page.
