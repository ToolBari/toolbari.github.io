Questions people ask

Is the CV Builder free?

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Is my information uploaded anywhere?

No. The builder runs entirely in your browser; what you type stays on your device and is never sent to a server.

What should a CV include?

Your name and job title, phone and email, a two-to-three-line summary, your work experience (most recent first), your skills, and your education. Keep it to one or two pages and match it to the job you are applying for.

How do I save my CV as a PDF?

Press the Print / Save as PDF button and choose "Save as PDF" in your browser's print window.
