How to use the Video Trimmer

Press Choose video file and pick a video from your device. The preview appears with a Play button and a seek slider, so you can watch the video and find the part you want to keep. The time display shows where you are in the video and how long the video is.

Type the start time and the end time in seconds. Decimals are allowed, so 10.5 means ten and a half seconds. The end time must be after the start time, and it cannot be later than the end of the video. Press Trim and export: the page plays the selected range and records it as it plays, and the progress bar shows how far the export has got. When the export finishes, a download link appears — press it to save the trimmed clip as a WebM file.

For example, a 60-second video clip with a start time of 10 seconds and an end time of 30 seconds gives a 20-second WebM download.

What happens during export

Export uses MediaRecorder with the video element's captureStream. The browser plays the selected range and records the picture and sound as they play, which is why export runs in real time: a 60-second clip takes about 60 seconds to export. The recording is saved as a WebM file, which is a different format from most original video files.

Because the clip is re-encoded, a small amount of quality loss is possible. The file never leaves your browser: it is played and recorded on your device, and nothing is uploaded. If your browser does not support MediaRecorder, the page shows a plain message instead of starting an export. Trimming cuts a time range out of a video, which is different from compressing a video to a smaller file.

Questions people ask

What format is the trimmed video?

The trimmed clip is downloaded as a WebM file. The video is re-encoded while it plays, so the download is not the original file or format, and a small amount of quality loss is possible.

How long does an export take?

Export runs in real time: a 20-second clip takes about 20 seconds to export, and a 60-second clip takes about 60 seconds. The progress bar shows how far the export has got.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. The file stays in your browser the whole time. It is played and recorded locally on your device, and nothing is sent to a server.

Why does the trimmer need a modern browser?

Trimming uses MediaRecorder and the video element's captureStream, which older browsers do not support. If your browser does not support them, the page shows a plain message instead of starting an export.
