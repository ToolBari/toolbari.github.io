Birthday Card Maker

Make a printable birthday card with a personal message in a few taps. Add the recipient's name, write your message, choose a theme, and download the card as an image to print or share.

The card is created in your browser and nothing is uploaded. For a printed card, use thicker paper and check the print preview before printing.

Writing a message that lands

The best card messages are short and specific. One warm sentence about the person, one wish for the year ahead, and your name is all a card needs. Mentioning a shared memory or something you admire about them makes the card feel personal instead of generic.

Keep the message to a few lines so it fits neatly on the card. If you have more to say, a letter or a long message in a chat is a better place for it.

Printing tips

The card is a tall image, 1080 by 1350 pixels. For printing, thicker paper or card stock feels much better than ordinary printer paper, and most print shops can print an image file on card for you.

Check the print preview before printing: some printers add margins that shrink the card, and a quick look at the preview saves a wasted sheet.

When to send the card

A card sent on the day itself is the classic choice, but a card that arrives a day or two early is a pleasant surprise and avoids the risk of a late delivery. If you are sharing the card digitally, sending it in the morning means it is one of the first birthday messages they see.

For someone far away, a digital card sent on time beats a paper card that arrives late. The downloaded image works in any messaging app, so distance is no reason to skip the card.

Questions people ask

What size is the card?

The card is 1080 by 1350 pixels, a tall image that suits sharing on a phone screen and printing at a small card size.

Can I leave the sender name blank?

Yes. The sender line is optional. If you leave it blank, the card simply shows the message without a sender name.

How do I print the card?

Use the Print button after making the card, or download the PNG and print it from your photo viewer or a print shop.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The card is drawn in your browser from what you type, and nothing leaves your device unless you share the downloaded image yourself.
