How to share these birthday photos

Find the photo for the birthday date, then save it to your phone or computer — long-press the photo on a phone, or right-click it and choose save on a computer. Then share it on WhatsApp, Facebook or Messenger, or send it as a message to the birthday person. All of the photos are free to share for personal use.

Questions people ask

Are these birthday photos free to download and share?

Yes, all of the November birthday photos are free to download and share for personal use. You can save the photo for the birthday date and send it to friends and family at no cost.

What dates in November are covered?

This page covers 15 dates, from 16 November to 30 November, with one photo for each day.

Can I share a photo on WhatsApp or Facebook?

Yes. Download the photo for the birthday date, then share it from your phone or computer on WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, or any messaging app you use.

Do the photos show a year?

No. Each photo shows the date and month only, so it works for a birthday in any year.
