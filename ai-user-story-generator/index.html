Add as many stories as you need. Each one appears in the list below the form, numbered in the order you added them. When the list is complete, copy it into your tracker or export it as a plain text file. To start over, press Clear list.

What makes a good user story

A good story names one user, one goal, and one benefit. If the goal contains the word and twice, it is probably two stories hiding in one sentence, and splitting them will make the work easier to plan. Keep the user specific: a new member behaves differently from an admin, and the story should show it.

The benefit is the part people skip, and it is the part that matters most. It explains why the work is worth doing, and it helps the team choose between two ways of building the same thing. Write the benefit from the user's point of view, not the business's.

Writing acceptance criteria that help

Acceptance criteria describe how you will know the story is done. The Given, When, Then form works well because it names a starting point, an action, and an expected result. A criterion you cannot test is a wish, not a check, so prefer concrete results over vague ones.

Always include the unhappy path: what should happen when something goes wrong. This tool adds one criterion for that case automatically. Read all three criteria after each story and rewrite any that do not match what your team would actually test.

Honest limits

This tool assembles stories from the words you enter. It cannot tell whether the goal is realistic, whether the benefit is real, or whether the criteria cover everything the story needs. Use the output as a draft, then discuss it with the people who will build and test it.

Questions people ask

What is the As a, I want, so that format?

It is a short sentence with three parts: who the user is, what they want to do, and why it matters to them. The format keeps the focus on the user instead of on the feature.

How many acceptance criteria should a story have?

Three is a good starting point: one for the main path, one for checking the result, and one for the unhappy path. Add more if the story has extra rules worth testing.

Can I edit a story after adding it?

The list keeps the stories you add in this session, in order. To change one, clear the list and add it again, or edit the exported text in your own document.

Does this tool save my stories anywhere?

No. Your stories stay in this browser tab and disappear when you close the page. Copy or export the list before you leave if you want to keep it.
