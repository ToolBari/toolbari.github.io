How to Start Freelancing

Short answer: pick one skill buyers already pay for, make 2–3 samples, open a profile with a clear photo and 3 packages, price the first gigs moderately, and deliver before the deadline. The guide below walks through each step, and the rate calculator works out what to charge.

Step 1 — Pick a skill buyers want

Choose a skill that people already pay for — video editing, thumbnail and graphic design, writing, or social media management. Open an account on Fiverr or Upwork and add a clear photo, a description of what you do, and a list of your skills. For your first gig, keep an easy-to-understand title, an eye-catching cover image, and 3 packages (Basic, Standard, Premium).

Step 2 — Win the first order

  • Keep your prices moderate at the start.
  • Reply to messages quickly — within 1 hour when you can.
  • Keep your best 2–3 samples of work on your profile.
  • Read the buyer's requirements carefully and send a custom offer.
  • On the first few orders, delivering before the deadline and earning good reviews is your real capital.

Step 3 — Get the money in hand

You can withdraw money from Fiverr to Payoneer, and from there directly to a Bangladeshi bank or bKash — the name must match exactly in both places. A new seller's earnings are usually released after 14 days. Fiverr charges a 20% fee on every order, so keep that in mind when you set your prices.

Work out your rate before you price a gig

Start from the monthly income you need, then divide by your working days and hours. The Hourly Rate Calculator does that sum: type your monthly income in BDT, your working days per month, your hours per day, and a target amount, and it shows the hourly rate that reaches it.
