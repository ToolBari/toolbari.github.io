Battery Status Checker

This checker reads your device battery straight from your browser and shows the charge level, whether it is charging, and the time estimates your browser reports. If your browser does not share battery information, the page says so plainly instead of guessing.

Press the button to read the battery status of this device. Nothing leaves your browser.

Readings come from your device and browser. Times are estimates where the browser reports them, and some browsers report no times at all. This page never guesses a number it cannot read.

What the checker shows

The charge level is the battery's remaining charge as a percentage, from 0 to 100. The charging state tells you whether the device is plugged in and charging right now. Where the browser reports them, you also see the time to a full charge while charging, or the time left on battery while unplugged. Times are shown in hours and minutes, so 5400 seconds appears as 1 h 30 min.

The readings refresh on their own when the level or the charging state changes, so you can leave the page open and watch the level move while the device charges or discharges. The numbers always come from the device you are using right now, so the same page can show different readings on a phone and on a desktop computer.

Why some browsers show nothing

Battery information comes from the Battery API, and not every browser provides it. Where the API is missing, this page shows an honest message and does not invent readings. Some browsers report the charge level and the charging state but no time estimates; in that case the page shows 'not reported' for the missing times instead of making one up.

Time estimates are only as good as the numbers the browser reports. A device that is working hard drains faster than one sitting idle, so a time-left figure is a rough guide at the moment you read it, not a promise. If a reading looks odd, press the button again to take a fresh reading.

Questions people ask

Why does this page show 'not reported' for the time?

Some browsers report the charge level and the charging state but no time estimates. The page shows 'not reported' for those values instead of inventing a time.

Does this page send my battery data anywhere?

No. The readings come from the Battery API in your browser and never leave your device.

Why does nothing show in my browser?

Not every browser exposes battery information. If the Battery API is missing, the page says so and does not guess.

Do the readings update while I watch?

Yes. When the charge level or the charging state changes, the page refreshes the readings automatically.
