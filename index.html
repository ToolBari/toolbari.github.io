    Common Fridge Problems and Care
    • Not cooling enough: Check whether the door rubber (gasket) seals properly and whether dust has built up on the rear coils; keep the fridge at least 4 inches away from the wall.
    • Ice forming inside: Don't keep the door open for long; don't put hot food in directly.
    • Too noisy: Check whether the fridge is on a level surface.
    • Once a month: Clean the inside with lukewarm water and baking soda.
    • If you suspect a gas/compressor problem, don't open it yourself — call a technician.
    Ceiling Fan Spinning Slowly? Check These First
    • Try setting the regulator to the highest step; an old, faulty regulator reduces speed.
    • Clean the dust built up on the blades (with the power off).
    • A weak capacitor makes the fan spin slowly — call an electrician to replace it; don't touch it yourself.
    • Noise or vibration in the shaft may indicate a bearing problem.
    Tips for Perfect Rice in a Rice Cooker
    • After washing the rice, measure the water: for new rice, water equal to the rice; for old rice, a little more. Water up to the first knuckle of your finger — a reliable measure.
    • Once the rice is cooked, keep the lid closed for 10 minutes, then stir.
    • Keep the outside of the inner pot dry — if it's wet, the heater gets damaged.

    Legal Document Information

    What documents you need: NID, passport, trade licence

    National Identity Card (NID) — New Registration and Correction

    New Registration: If you are 18 or older, you can apply at the Upazila/Thana Election Office or online. Usually required: birth certificate, educational certificate (SSC/equivalent, if any), copies of parents' NID, proof of address (electricity bill/holding tax receipt), citizenship certificate (where applicable).

    Correction/Duplicate: Online services.nidw.gov.bd you can apply; for corrections, you must upload supporting documents for your claim (the SSC certificate is given priority, birth certificate, passport, etc.). The prescribed government fee applies.

    e-Passport — Application Steps

    1. epassport.gov.bdcreate an account and fill in the online application (information exactly as per NID/birth certificate). 2. Pay the fee online or at a bank — the fee varies by number of pages, validity and delivery type; the latest fee list is on the site. 3. On the scheduled date, visit the passport office for biometrics, photo and iris scan. 4. Keep the delivery slip; when the passport is ready you will receive an SMS, and you can collect it by showing the slip. You can also check the application status on the same site.

    What you need: NID (mandatory for adults) or online birth certificate (English version), old passport (for renewal), a recent utility bill as proof of address, and relevant certificates/attestations for professionals.

    Birth Registration — New and Correction

    Registration should be done within 45 days of birth. Apply: at the registrar office of the Union Parishad/Municipality/City Corporation or bdris.gov.bdonline. Usually required: hospital birth documents/vaccination card, parents' birth certificates and NID. An English version of the birth certificate is needed for many purposes including passports — make sure to provide the English information correctly when applying.

    Trade Licence — For Shops/Businesses

    To start a new business, you need a trade licence from the local City Corporation/Municipality/Union Parishad. Usually required: the owner's NID and photo, shop rent agreement or proof of ownership, the previous year's licence (for renewal). The licence must be renewed every fiscal year. Fees vary by business type.

    Driving Licence — Steps

    1. BRTAapply for a learner (trainee) licence. 2. After the prescribed period, take the written, oral and practical (field) tests. 3.     Recipes & Lifestyle

    Bangladeshi pitha recipes and lifestyle tips

    🥞 Chitoi Pitha

    Ingredients: Rice flour 2 cups, lukewarm water 2.5 cups, salt to taste.

    Method: Mix the rice flour and salt with warm water to make a medium-thin batter (slightly thicker than dal). Heat an earthen pan or iron wok well and wipe it with a little oil. Pour in one ladleful of batter, cover with a lid and steam for 2–3 minutes — when tiny holes appear on top, the pitha is ready. Eat it hot with mustard bhorta, coriander bhorta or molasses.

    ❄️ Bhapa Pitha

    Ingredients: Rice flour 3 cups, date molasses 1 cup (crumbled), grated coconut 1 cup, a little salt, a little water for sprinkling.

    Method: Sprinkle salt and a little water over the rice flour and rub it into crumbs, then sift it through a sieve. In a small bowl/mould, layer flour first, then molasses and coconut in the middle, then flour again on top. Wrap it in a thin cloth, set it over the mouth of a pot of boiling water and steam for 5–7 minutes. Serve hot.

    🌯 Patishapta Pitha

    Ingredients: For the batter — rice flour 1 cup, all-purpose flour half a cup, sugar 2 tablespoons, milk/water 2 cups. For the filling — milk 1 litre, molasses or sugar half a cup, grated coconut half a cup.

    Method: Boil the milk down to make a thick kheer, then mix in the molasses/sugar and coconut to make the filling. Make a thin batter; brush a little ghee/oil on a flat pan, spread one ladleful of batter and cook it like a thin crepe. Place the filling in the middle, fold in from both sides and take it off the pan.

    Freelancing Guide

    Start freelancing: gigs, orders and getting paid

    1. How to get started

    Choose a skill that people want to buy — video editing, thumbnail/graphic design, writing, or social media management. Open an account on Fiverr or Upwork and add a clear photo to your profile, a description of what you do, and a list of your skills. For your first gig, keep: an easy-to-understand title, an eye-catching cover image, and 3 packages (Basic/Standard/Premium).

    2. How to get your first order

    If you are a beginner, keep your prices moderate at the start; reply to messages quickly (within 1 hour); keep your best 2–3 samples of work on your profile; read the buyer's requirements carefully and send a custom offer; and on the first few orders, delivering before the deadline and earning good reviews is your real capital.

    3. How you will get the money in hand

    You can withdraw money from Fiverr to Payoneer, and from there directly to a Bangladeshi bank or bKash (the name must match exactly in both places). A new seller's earnings are usually released after 14 days. Fiverr charges a 20% fee on every order — keep that in mind when setting your prices.

    4. Beware of scams

    Asking you to take payment outside the platform, asking for money in advance, or asking for your password/code — these are signs of a scam. Never share your password or verification code with anyone. Keep all communication and payments inside the platform.

    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 2026

    ToolBari — All In One Tools ("we", "this site") is a collection of free browser-based utilities. We respect your privacy. This page explains what data is — and is not — collected when you use this site.

    Files you process

    Almost every tool on this site (image tools, PDF maker, calculators, text tools, QR generator and others) runs entirely inside your own browser. Images, documents and text you use with these tools are never uploaded to or stored on our servers — we do not even operate a file server for them.

    Tools that use third-party services

    A small number of tools send the specific link you paste to a third-party public service in order to function: the Video Downloader (a relay service we operate, which forwards the pasted link to a download backend), the TikTok Downloader (TikWM), the Short Link Generator (spoo.me), the Breaking News tool (news RSS feeds via AllOrigins), the Stock Exchange tool (TradingView) and the Instagram DP Viewer (Unavatar). The Currency Converter fetches public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API, Prayer Times uses the Aladhan API, Weather uses Open-Meteo, the IP & Device tool uses public IP-information services, and the DNS Checker queries Google Public DNS. Only the URL or username you enter is sent, only when you press the button. Those services have their own privacy policies.

    Browser storage

    Your starred "favorite tools" are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. No account is created and no personal profile is built.

    Advertising

    This site may display advertisements from third-party ad networks (such as Adsterra or Google AdSense). Ad networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can control cookies in your browser settings at any time.

    Children

    This site is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy? See the Contact page.
