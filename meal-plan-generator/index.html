Questions people ask

What does the Meal Plan Generator give me?

A 7-day week — Monday to Sunday — with one simple main-meal idea per day. You choose between two diets: Any (includes chicken, fish and beef) or Vegetarian (dal, vegetables, paneer and egg-free options).

What does a sample day look like?

Day 1 (Monday) of the Any plan is: Rice, chicken and salad. The full Any week runs: rice, chicken and salad · rice, fish and vegetables · khichuri and chicken · roti, beef and salad · rice, fish curry and vegetables · biriyani · rice, chicken and dal.

Does the plan include breakfast, lunch and dinner separately?

No. The generator gives one main-meal idea for each day of the week — it does not split the day into breakfast, lunch and dinner, and it does not count calories or portions.

Is this meal plan medical or diet advice?

No. These are general meal ideas only, not medical or diet advice. If you have diabetes, allergies or any health condition, follow your doctor's or dietitian's guidance, not a generated list.
