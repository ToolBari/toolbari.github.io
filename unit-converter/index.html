Questions people ask

How many kilograms are in a maund?

One maund (মণ), the traditional Bangladeshi unit, equals 37.3242 kilograms. So 10 maund of rice is 373.242 kg — just type it into the weight converter above.

How do I convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32, then multiply by 5/9. For example, 100 °F = (100 − 32) × 5/9 = 37.7778 °C. The temperature converter above does it in one tap, including Kelvin.
