Username Generator

Find a username that fits you. Enter a keyword, pick a style and length, and get a list of fresh username ideas you can copy and try wherever you need one.

These are ideas only. This tool cannot check whether a name is taken, so try the name on the site or app where you want to use it.

How the ideas are made

Each idea combines your keyword with a word from the style you picked, sometimes with a number or a separator such as an underscore or a dot. No idea contains a space, and every idea stays within the length you chose.

Pressing Generate again shuffles the combinations, so you can keep going until something catches your eye. With no keyword, the ideas are built from the style words alone.

Picking a good username

Short names are easier to remember, type and say out loud. Avoid long strings of numbers, which are hard to remember and look like a throwaway account. A name that hints at what you do or like is easier for other people to recognise later.

Before settling on a name, try it on the site or app where you will use it, and think about how it reads to someone who does not know you. A name that is easy to spell avoids a lot of confusion.

Usernames and privacy

Think twice before building a username from your real name, your birth year or your town. Those details make you easier to identify, and once a name is in use across many sites it is hard to take back. A keyword you like, rather than a fact about you, keeps things lighter.

Using a different username on each site also limits how easily your accounts can be linked together. The generator makes it quick to come up with a fresh one whenever you need it.

Questions people ask

Does it check availability?

No. The generator only creates ideas. Whether a name is free depends on the site or app, so try the name there to find out.

Can I use it without a keyword?

Yes. Leave the keyword blank and the ideas are built from the style words alone, with numbers and separators mixed in.

Why do some names have numbers?

Numbers help when a plain name is already taken, and they make short ideas more varied. You can ignore the numbered ideas and use a plain one instead.

Can I generate again?

Yes. Press Generate as many times as you like. Each press shuffles the combinations and produces a new list of ideas.
