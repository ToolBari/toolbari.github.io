How to use the tone generator

Enter a frequency between 20 and 20000 Hz, pick a waveform, set a duration between 0.1 and 60 seconds, and press Play tone. The tone plays for the set duration and then stops by itself. Press Stop at any time to end it early.

The volume slider controls how loud the tone plays. Start low and raise it gradually, especially when using headphones.

What the waveforms sound like

A sine wave is the purest tone, a single pitch with no extra harmonics. A triangle wave sounds soft and flute-like. A square wave sounds hollow and buzzy, and a sawtooth wave sounds bright and buzzy.

Tones are handy for comparing how speakers or headphones handle different pitches, for checking that left and right channels both work, and as a simple tuning reference.

Testing speakers with tones

Play tones from low to high and listen for rattles, buzzes or notes that suddenly get much quieter — those are signs a speaker is struggling at that pitch. Keep the volume modest while you do this.

To compare two speakers or two pairs of headphones, play the same frequency on each in turn at the same volume setting. Differences in loudness or tone tell you how they handle that pitch. If a tone sounds different in one ear than the other, try swapping the earbuds or checking the balance setting on your device.

Questions people ask

What frequency should I start with?

440 Hz is a good starting point because it sits in the middle of the range most speakers handle well. Move up or down from there to explore.

Why can I not hear very low or very high tones?

Small speakers and headphones often cannot reproduce the lowest and highest frequencies, and hearing varies from person to person. The tone may still be playing even if you cannot hear it.

Which waveform sounds the purest?

A sine wave, because it contains a single pitch with no extra harmonics. Square and sawtooth waves add harmonics that make them sound harsher.

Does the tone keep playing if I leave the page?

No. The tone stops when the set duration ends, when you press Stop, or when you leave the page.
