How it works

  1. Type the topic of your post — for example, "birthday cake".
  2. Pick the platform the post is for.
  3. Add the video topic if the post is a video.
  4. Pick the length.
  5. Press Generate Caption.

Example caption

A fresh birthday cake, made to order — soft, sweet, and ready for the candles. Tag someone whose birthday is coming up!

Example only, for a birthday-cake post. Your caption will follow the topic, platform and length you pick.

Make the post work harder

  • Hashtags — pair the caption with tags from the Hashtag Generator so people can find the post.
  • Tracking — if the post carries a link, tag it with the UTM Builder so you can see which post brought the visits.
  • Your own words — edit the caption before posting; one true detail about your own post beats any ready-made line.

Questions people ask

What do I type into the Social Caption Generator?

Type your topic, pick the platform, add the video topic if the post is a video, and pick the length. Then press Generate Caption.

Can I use the captions on any platform?

Yes. Pick the platform in the Platform box, and the caption is shaped for it. You can edit the words before you post.

Should I still add hashtags?

Hashtags help people find the post. Pair the caption with tags from the Hashtag Generator on ToolBari.

Is the Social Caption Generator free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, and it runs in your browser on any phone or computer.
