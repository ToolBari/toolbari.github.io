What the result means

  • Total days — the exact elapsed days between the two calendar dates.
  • Years, months and days — the calendar breakdown, borrowing the correct number of days from the previous month when needed.
  • Weeks — total days divided by 7, shown to one decimal place.
  • Approximate months — total days divided by 30.44, the average month length used by the full ToolBari calculator.

Worked examples

  1. 15 Jan 2025 → 20 Mar 2025: 64 days = 0 years, 2 months, 5 days.
  2. 1 Jan 2024 → 1 Jan 2025: 366 days because 2024 is a leap year.
  3. 29 Feb 2024 → 28 Feb 2025: 365 days = 0 years, 11 months, 30 days.

Questions people ask

How many days are between 15 January and 20 March 2025?

There are 64 days between 15 January 2025 and 20 March 2025 — exactly 0 years, 2 months and 5 days, or 9.1 weeks.

Does the order of the dates matter?

No. The calculator sorts the two dates automatically, so entering the later date first gives the same difference.

Does the date difference count both the first and last day?

It counts elapsed time between the dates, so 1 January to 2 January is 1 day. If you are counting event days and want both endpoints included, add 1 to the total.

How does it handle leap years?

It uses the real calendar, including 29 February. From 29 February 2024 to 28 February 2025 is 365 days, shown as 0 years, 11 months and 30 days.
