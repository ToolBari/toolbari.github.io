How to share these birthday photos

Find the photo for the birthday date and save it to your device — long-press the photo on a phone, or right-click it on a computer and choose save. Then send it as a message or post it on WhatsApp, Facebook, or Messenger. The photos are free to share for personal use, so you can send one to as many March birthdays as you like.

Questions people ask

Are these birthday photos free to download and share?

Yes. Every March birthday photo on this page is free to download and share for personal use — no sign-up and no payment needed.

What dates in March are covered?

All of them. The gallery has one birthday photo for each day from 1 March to 31 March, so every March birthday is covered.

Can I share a photo on WhatsApp or Facebook?

Yes. Download the photo for the birthday date, then share it from your phone or computer on WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, or any app you use.

Do the photos show a year?

No. Each photo shows the date and month only, so the same photo works every year.
