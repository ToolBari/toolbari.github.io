Barbell Plate Calculator

Enter the total weight you want on the bar and the weight of the bar itself, and this tool works out exactly which plates go on each side. It lists the plates from heaviest to lightest, which is also the order to load them.

Load the same plates on both sides and check the bar is balanced before lifting. This tool only does the arithmetic — it does not judge whether a weight is safe for you to lift.

How to use the plate calculator

Enter the total weight you want on the bar, then the weight of the bar itself, and choose kilograms or pounds. Press Calculate plates to see the plates for one side of the bar, listed in the order to load them.

The kilogram plate set is 25, 20, 15, 10, 5, 2.5 and 1.25. The pound plate set is 45, 35, 25, 10, 5 and 2.5. The bar weight is not fixed — enter the weight of the bar you are actually using.

How the plates are worked out

First the bar weight is subtracted from the target weight, then the result is halved, because the plates are split equally between the two sides. The tool then fills one side with the largest plates first until the weight is reached.

Some targets cannot be made exactly from the listed plates. In that case the tool shows the closest load it can make and how far short of the target it is, so you can adjust the target or use smaller plates if you have them.

Loading the bar safely

Load the heaviest plate first on each side, then the lighter ones outside it, and finish with collars so nothing slides. Count the plates on both sides before you lift — an uneven bar can tip.

When you unload, take the lightest plates off first and alternate sides so the bar stays balanced. Never pull all the plates off one side while the other side is still loaded.

Questions people ask

What does plates per side mean?

The plates listed go on one side of the bar. You need two of each plate listed, one for each side, to keep the bar balanced.

Why load the heaviest plate first?

The heaviest plate goes on first, closest to the centre of the bar, with lighter plates outside it. The result lists plates in that loading order.

What if my target weight cannot be made exactly?

The tool shows the closest load the listed plates can make and how far short of the target it is. Change the target slightly or add smaller plates if you have them.

Does the bar weight count towards the total?

Yes. The target weight is the total on the bar including the bar itself, so the tool subtracts the bar weight before working out the plates.
