Worked example

Gross salary 50,000 a month, 2 festival bonus months, no PF, tax included:

  1. Yearly gross: 50,000 × 12 = 6,00,000; bonuses add 50,000 × 2 = 1,00,000 — total income 7,00,000.
  2. Estimated tax (FY 2024–25 slabs): first 3,50,000 tax-free; next 1,00,000 at 5% = 5,000; remaining 2,50,000 at 10% = 25,000 — total tax 30,000.
  3. Net year: 7,00,000 − 30,000 = 6,70,000 → average take-home 55,833 a month; hourly rate 50,000 ÷ 208 ≈ 240.4.

Income tax is estimated according to the individual slabs for the fiscal year 2024–25 (general taxpayer) — the same basis as the full ToolBari tool. Investment rebates, minimum tax and tax deducted at source are not included, and actual salary structures vary by organisation.

Questions people ask

How is monthly take-home pay calculated here?

Yearly gross (12 × monthly salary) plus festival bonuses, minus provident fund deduction and estimated income tax, divided by 12. For example, a 50,000 monthly salary with 2 bonus months and tax included gives a 670,000 net year — an average of 55,833 take-home per month.

Which income tax slabs does the calculator use?

The Bangladesh individual slabs for fiscal year 2024–25 for a general taxpayer: the first 3,50,000 of yearly income is tax-free, then 5% on the next 1,00,000, 10% on the next 3,00,000, 15% on the next 4,00,000, 20% on the next 5,00,000 and 25% above that. It is an estimate — investment rebates, minimum tax and tax deducted at source are not included.

How is the hourly rate worked out?

The monthly gross salary is divided by 208 — that is 26 working days at 8 hours a day. A 50,000 monthly salary therefore equals about 240.4 per hour before deductions.
