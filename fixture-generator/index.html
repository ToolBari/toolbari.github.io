Fixture Generator

Enter your team names, one per line, and this tool builds a round-robin schedule where every team plays every other team once, or twice with a double round. Fixtures are grouped by round and the list is ready to print.

With an odd number of teams, one team sits out each round and is shown as having a bye. Dates, times and venues are not set here — add those when you share the schedule.

How to make fixtures

Enter one team name per line, choose a single or double round, and press Generate fixtures. A single round means every pair of teams meets once. A double round means every pair meets twice, once at each team's home, so the schedule is twice as long.

Duplicate names are removed, and blank lines are ignored. The schedule is grouped by round in playing order, and the Print button prints the whole list.

How round-robin scheduling works

In each round every team plays exactly one match, so nobody plays twice in the same round. With an even number of teams, a single round has one round fewer than the number of teams. With an odd number of teams, one team has a bye in each round.

The total number of matches in a single round is the number of teams multiplied by one less than the number of teams, divided by two. A double round doubles both the rounds and the matches.

Sharing the schedule with your teams

Once the fixtures are generated, print the list or copy it into a message for the teams. Add dates, times and venues as you go, since the tool only decides who plays whom and in which round.

Home and away sides are balanced as evenly as the schedule allows. If your league needs exact home and away counts, check the generated list and swap the sides of a fixture where needed.

Questions people ask

How many teams can I enter?

Two or more. There is no fixed upper limit, but very long lists make very long schedules, so keep the list to the teams actually playing.

What is a bye?

When the number of teams is odd, one team has no opponent in a round. That team is shown as having a bye for that round and plays again in the next one.

What changes with a double round?

Every fixture is played twice, once at each team's home, so the schedule has twice as many rounds and matches as a single round.

Can I change the fixtures after generating them?

Edit the team list and generate again. To move a single match, print the schedule and adjust it by hand.
