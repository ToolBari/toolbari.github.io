💝 Hambalyo Daacad ah

Dhalasho wacan! Sannadkan ha kuu keeno caafimaad, nabad iyo wax kasta oo qalbigaagu jecel yahay.Happy birthday! May this year bring you health, peace and everything your heart loves.
Maanta waa maalintii uu dhashay qofka aan dhammaanteen jecel nahay. Ku raaxayso daqiiqad kasta oo maalintaada ah!Today is the day the person we all love was born. Enjoy every minute of your day!
Sannad kasta oo dhaafa, joogitaankaagu wuxuu noqonayaa mid ka qiimo badan noo ah. Dhalasho wacan!With every passing year, your presence becomes more precious to us. Happy birthday!
Dhalashadaadu ha noqoto mid iftiin leh sida dhoollacadayntaada iyo mid deeqsinimo leh sida qalbigaaga.May your birthday be as bright as your smile and as generous as your heart.
Waad ku mahadsan tahay inaad mar walba nala joogto. Maanta maalintu adaa iska leh — dhalasho wacan!Thank you for always being with us. Today the day is yours — happy birthday!
Farxadda aad dadka kale siiso ha kuu soo noqoto labo jibaar noloshaada, maanta iyo weligeedba.May the happiness you give to others come back doubled into your life, today and always.
Riyooyinkaagu ha bilaabaan inay rumoobaan, mid mid, laga bilaabo sannadkan. Dhalasho wacan!May your dreams begin to come true, one by one, starting this year. Happy birthday!
Nolosheennu way ka dhammaystiran tahay joogitaankaaga darteed. Dhalasho wacan, jacayl oo dhan!Our life is more complete because of you. Happy birthday, with all our love!

Sidee baan u hambalyeynayaa dhalasho af Soomaali?

Ku bilow «Dhalasho wacan!» kadibna ku dar hal jumlad oo qalbiga ka timid — xusuus aad wadaagteen, mahadnaq, ama rabitaan caafimaad iyo farxad. Farriin gaaban oo daacad ah ayaa ka taabanaysa badan qoraal dheer oo la koobiyeyay.

Farriin gaaban oo dhalasho ah oo aan WhatsApp ku diri karaa waa tee?
