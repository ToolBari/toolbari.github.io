❓ Questions people ask

How do I calculate my exact age?

Enter your date of birth in the calculator above. It shows your exact age in years, months and days, the total number of days you have lived, and the weekday you were born on.

How many days until my next birthday?

The calculator counts down to your next birthday automatically and shows the date, how many days remain, and the age you will turn.

Does the age calculator handle leap-year birthdays?

Yes. If you were born on 29 February, your birthday in a non-leap year is counted on 28 February, and the age calculation stays exact.
