✍️ What to write in the card

Name the couple, wish them one specific thing — a peaceful home, patience in the hard weeks, laughter in old age — and sign off with warmth. Three honest sentences beat a page of poetry copied without meaning it.

❓ Questions people ask

What is a good wedding wish?

A good wedding wish names the couple and wishes them one true thing: 'Congratulations — may your home be full of peace and your love grow stronger every year.' Specific and sincere beats long and copied.

What do you write in a wedding card?

Three short parts: congratulations to both by name, one specific hope for their marriage, and a warm sign-off from you. If you are attending, add one line about the day itself — it proves the card was written for them, not copied for anyone.

What is a short wedding wish for WhatsApp?

Short and warm wins on WhatsApp: 'Congratulations! Wishing you a lifetime of love and laughter.' Or with a blessing: 'Barakallahu lakuma — may Allah bless your marriage.' Both fit in a single message, with room for a photo of the couple.
