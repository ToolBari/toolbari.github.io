💝 Matakwa ya Dhati

Hereri ya kuzaliwa! Mwaka huu ukuletee afya, amani na kila kitu moyo wako unachotamani zaidi.Happy birthday! May this year bring you health, peace and everything your heart wishes for most.
Leo ni siku aliyezaliwa mtu anayependwa sana na sisi sote. Furahia kila dakika ya siku yako!Today is the day someone loved very much by all of us was born. Enjoy every minute of your day!
Kila mwaka unavyopita, uwepo wako unazidi kuwa wa thamani zaidi kwetu. Hereri ya kuzaliwa!With every passing year, your presence becomes even more precious to us. Happy birthday!
Siku yako ya kuzaliwa iwe angavu kama tabasamu lako na karimu kama moyo wako.May your birthday be as bright as your smile and as generous as your heart.
Asante kwa kuwa pamoja nasi kila wakati. Leo siku ni yako kabisa — hereri ya kuzaliwa!Thank you for always being with us. Today the day is completely yours — happy birthday!
Furaha yote unayowapa wengine ikurudie mara mbili katika maisha yako, leo na kila siku.May all the happiness you give others come back doubled into your life, today and every day.
Ndoto zako zianze kutimia, moja baada ya nyingine, kuanzia mwaka huu. Hereri ya kuzaliwa!May your dreams begin to come true, one after another, starting this year. Happy birthday!
Maisha yetu yamekamilika zaidi kwa sababu ya uwepo wako. Hereri ya kuzaliwa, kwa upendo wote!Our life is more complete because of your presence. Happy birthday, with all our love!

Nisemaje hereri ya kuzaliwa kwa Kiswahili?

Ni ujumbe gani mfupi wa siku ya kuzaliwa ninaoweza kutuma kwa WhatsApp?

Ujumbe mfupi na wa joto unatosha — «Hereri ya kuzaliwa! Afya, furaha na mafanikio!» Kwenye ukurasa huu kuna matakwa 25 ya siku ya kuzaliwa tayari kunakiliwa na kutumwa, kila moja likiwa na maana kwa Kiingereza.
