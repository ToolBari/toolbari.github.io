✍️ What to write in the card

For a brother, keep it short and strong: thank him for one real thing — protection, help, a loan of his bike — then wish him success. Brothers trust actions more than poetry, so let the words sound like actions too.

❓ Questions people ask

What is a heartfelt birthday wish for a brother?

Thank him for something real: 'Thank you for every time you stood between me and trouble. Happy birthday, bhai — may life repay you double.'

How do I wish my elder brother respectfully?

Use 'bhai' or his respectful name, thank him for his guidance, and pray for his health and success. An Islamic option: 'Barakallahu fi umrik.'
