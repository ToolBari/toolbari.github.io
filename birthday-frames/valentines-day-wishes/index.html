What is a romantic Valentine's Day message?

'Every day with you feels like a love story I never want to finish.' One true line about your own story beats a page of borrowed poetry.

What is a short Valentine's message for WhatsApp?

'You are my today and all my tomorrows.' Short enough for a status, warm enough for a card — add a name-on-cake photo to make it a gift.

Is there a respectful Valentine's wish for someone special?

Yes — choose a sweet, blessing-style line such as 'Happy Valentine's Day to my partner in duas and dreams', and keep the message kind and modest.
