AI Bug Report Generator

This tool turns your notes into a clear, complete bug report with numbered steps to reproduce, expected and actual behaviour, environment and severity. Fill in what you saw, then copy the finished report into your issue tracker.

A good report describes what you saw, not what you guess caused it. Leave the cause to the person fixing the bug. This tool works fully in your browser and calls no AI service, API, or backend. It formats the details you enter; the facts are yours.

How to use this generator

Give the bug a short title that says what is wrong, then write the steps to reproduce, one step per line. Add what you expected to happen and what actually happened, pick the platform, browser and severity, and press the button.

The tool numbers your steps and assembles a clean report you can paste into your issue tracker. Read it once before you send it, and check that someone who has never seen the bug could follow the steps and reach the same result.

What makes a bug report easy to fix

One bug per report is the most important rule. Two bugs in one report get fixed at the speed of the slower one, and the conversation gets confused. If you found two problems, write two reports.

Exact steps matter more than long descriptions. Write each click, each entry, and each page in order, starting from a fresh start. If the bug only appears sometimes, say so, and say how often you saw it. Screenshots help, but they never replace written steps.

Choosing a severity

Critical means people cannot do their work at all, or data is lost. High means a main feature is broken but there is a painful way around it. Medium means something is wrong but a reasonable workaround exists. Low means the problem is cosmetic or affects few people.

When you are unsure between two levels, pick the lower one and explain why in the report. Severity is a starting point for the person triaging the bug, not a final judgement, and they can raise it if they disagree.

Honest limits

This tool formats what you enter. It cannot reproduce the bug, check whether the steps are complete, or judge the severity for you. If the person fixing the bug asks for more detail, add it to the report rather than starting a new thread.

Keep private information out of bug reports: no passwords, no personal data, no internal links that outsiders should not see. If the bug involves real user data, describe it in general terms.

Questions people ask

Why are steps to reproduce required?

Without steps, the person fixing the bug has to guess how to reach it, and often cannot. Steps turn a complaint into something a developer can reproduce, test, and confirm as fixed.

What should I put in Expected and Actual?

Expected is what the software should have done. Actual is what it did instead. One sentence each is enough, and the contrast between them is what makes the bug clear.

How do I pick the right severity?

Judge by impact: blocked work or lost data is critical, a broken main feature is high, a problem with a workaround is medium, and a cosmetic issue is low. When unsure, pick the lower level and explain why.

Does this tool file the bug for me?

No. It builds the report text for you to copy into your own issue tracker. Nothing is sent anywhere from this page.
