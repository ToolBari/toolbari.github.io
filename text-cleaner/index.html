Text Cleaner

Paste messy text and this cleaner tidies it in one pass: double spaces become single, trailing spaces are removed, extra blank lines are trimmed, and smart quotes become straight quotes. The clean text is ready to copy.

The cleaner changes spacing and punctuation marks, which is what most pasted text needs, but it cannot fix spelling or wording. Read the result through once before using it somewhere important.

What the cleaner fixes

Text pasted from documents, web pages and messages often arrives with small messes. This cleaner fixes them in one pass: runs of two or more spaces become a single space, spaces at the ends of lines are removed, and tabs become single spaces. A space before a full stop, comma or question mark is removed, so sentences read normally again.

Blank lines are tidied too: three or more line breaks in a row become a single blank line, so paragraphs stay separated without large gaps. Smart quotes, the curly opening and closing marks many word processors insert, become straight quotes, which behave better in plain text files, code and forms.

What it leaves alone

The cleaner only changes spacing, blank lines and a few punctuation marks. Your words, their order, their spelling and your numbers stay exactly as you pasted them. Line breaks between paragraphs are kept, and the result keeps the same paragraphs in the same order.

The stats line above the result shows how many characters the text had before and after cleaning, so you can see how much tidying was done. Copy the clean text when you are happy with it.

Messy spacing is common in text copied from PDFs, where each line can end with a stray space, and in text pasted from chat apps, where extra blank lines creep in. Running the text through the cleaner first saves fixing the same small problems by hand.

Questions people ask

What are smart quotes and why change them?

Smart quotes are the curly opening and closing marks many word processors insert. They look fine in documents but can cause trouble in code, plain text files and some forms, so the cleaner swaps them for straight quotes.

Does the cleaner change my words?

No. It only changes spacing, blank lines and a few punctuation marks. Your words, their order and their spelling stay exactly as you pasted them.

How many blank lines are kept?

Runs of blank lines are reduced to a single blank line, so paragraphs stay separated without large gaps. Single blank lines between paragraphs are left alone.

Can I clean text with tabs in it?

Yes. Tab characters are turned into single spaces, which keeps columns of pasted text readable without the wide gaps tabs can cause.
