Questions people ask

How is an invoice total calculated?

Add quantity × unit price for every item. Apply discount to the subtotal, then VAT or tax to the discounted amount.

What is the total on the sample invoice?

৳12,500 − 10% discount (৳1,250) + 5% tax on ৳11,250 (৳562.50) = ৳11,812.50; after ৳5,000 paid, due is ৳6,812.50.
