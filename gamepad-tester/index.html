The tester only reads what the browser's Gamepad API reports. Some gamepads need a button press before the browser sees them, and some browsers do not support vibration at all, so a missing feature here does not always mean the gamepad is broken.

How to connect a gamepad

Plug a wired gamepad in with its cable, or pair a wireless gamepad with your device's Bluetooth settings first. Then press any button on the gamepad: most browsers only list a gamepad once it has sent input, so a connected but untouched gamepad stays invisible.

If nothing appears, press Scan again, reload the page, or try another cable or port. Gamepads that work in one browser usually work in others too, so trying a second browser is a quick way to tell whether the problem is the gamepad or the browser.

What the display shows

Buttons light up as you press them, numbered in the order the browser reports them. Button 0 is usually the bottom face button on a standard layout, and the shoulder buttons and triggers follow after the face buttons and the menu buttons.

Each axis reports a value from minus 1 to 1. A centred stick reads near zero on both of its axes, and pushing a stick to an edge moves the matching axis towards minus 1 or 1. If a stick rests far from zero, it may be drifting and need cleaning or repair.

Questions people ask

Why does my gamepad not show up?

Press any button on the gamepad after connecting it, because most browsers only list a gamepad once it has sent input. If it still does not appear, check the cable or Bluetooth pairing and reload the page.

What do the button numbers mean?

Buttons are numbered in the order the browser reports them, following the standard gamepad layout. Button 0 is usually the bottom face button, and the exact layout depends on your controller.

What do the axis numbers mean?

Each axis reports a value from -1 to 1. A centred stick reads near 0 on both axes, and pushing a stick to an edge moves the matching axis towards -1 or 1.

Why does vibration not work on my gamepad?

Vibration needs a gamepad with rumble motors and a browser that supports the vibration effect. If either is missing, the page says so instead of pretending the test ran.
