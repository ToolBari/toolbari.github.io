Calendar Maker

Make a printable calendar for any month or any full year. Pick the year, the month and the first day of the week, add notes to the dates that matter, then print it or download it.

This calendar shows dates only. It does not include public holidays, so add any holidays you need as notes.

How to make your calendar

Start by typing the year you want, then choose a month if you only need one page, or pick the yearly view to see all twelve months at once. The week can start on Sunday or Monday, whichever matches the calendars you normally use. When you press Make calendar, the grid is drawn with the correct number of days for that month, including 29 February in a leap year, and today is highlighted when the calendar shows the current month.

Notes turn a plain calendar into your own. Each note sits under the date it belongs to, so birthdays, appointments, deadlines and reminders are visible at a glance. You can add as many notes as you like, and several notes can share the same date. If a line does not follow the date format, it is skipped and the message under the buttons tells you how many lines were skipped, so nothing is silently lost.

Notes and printing tips

Write each note on its own line, starting with the full date, then a colon, then the note itself. For example, the line 2026-03-14: Family dinner puts the words Family dinner under 14 March 2026. Dates must be real calendar dates, so 2026-02-30 would be skipped because February never has 30 days. Notes work in both views: in the yearly view each note appears in its own month.

To print, press Print and use your browser print dialog. A monthly calendar fits on one page, and the yearly view prints month by month. The Download button saves the calendar as a file you can open in any browser and print later. The calendar is made entirely on this page, so your notes never leave your device.

Questions people ask

Which years can I make a calendar for?

Any year from 1900 to 2100. Type the year, choose a month or the full year, and press Make calendar.

How do I add a note to a date?

Write one note per line with the date first, then a colon, then the note, for example 2026-03-14: Family dinner. The note appears under that date on the calendar.

Can I start the week on Monday?

Yes. Choose Monday or Sunday as the first day of the week before you make the calendar.

How do I print the calendar?

Press Print to open your browser print dialog, then choose your printer or save as PDF.
