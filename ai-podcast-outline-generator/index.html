How to use the podcast outline generator

Type your episode topic, pick a format, and pick a length from 15 to 60 minutes. The tool builds an outline with a cold open, three main segments with time splits, and an outro, plus four questions to ask. Press Regenerate variation to rotate the questions and see a different arrangement. Copy the outline when it fits your show.

The time splits are worked out from your chosen length: a short cold open, most of the time on the three segments, and a short outro. The splits always add up to the full episode length, so you can read the clock during recording and know whether you are on pace.

The four formats

Solo episodes suit a host with a clear message: one voice, one idea, three supporting points. Interview episodes suit a guest with a story: the segments follow their journey, and the questions draw out turning points and advice. Co-hosted episodes suit two voices who can agree and disagree: the outline gives each host room to take a side. Storytelling episodes suit a narrative: a scene, a twist, and a lesson.

Pick the format before you plan the segments, because the format decides what the segments should do. A solo outline used for an interview will leave the guest with nothing to answer, and an interview outline used solo will sound like talking to an empty chair.

Planning time splits that hold

Most episodes run long in the first segment and rush the last one. The outline guards against that by giving each segment a clock range, so you can see the time as you record. If the first segment runs two minutes over, you know to trim the second by two minutes, not to rush the outro.

Leave the outro alone when time runs short. The outro is where listeners hear what to do next and where to find you, and a rushed outro wastes the whole episode. Cut a segment question instead, and keep the closing calm and complete.

Questions people ask

What does the outline include?

A cold open, three main segments with clock time splits, an outro, and four questions to ask. The time splits always add up to the episode length you picked.

Can I change the segments?

Yes. The outline is a template-built starting point. Rename segments, move time between them, and rewrite the questions to fit your show.

Which format should I pick?

Pick the format that matches how you record: solo for one voice, interview for a guest, co-hosted for two hosts, and storytelling for a narrative episode.

Does the tool save my outline?

No. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored. Copy the outline before you leave the page.
