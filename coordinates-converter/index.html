Degrees, minutes and seconds

One degree is 60 minutes and one minute is 60 seconds, so one degree is 3600 seconds. To turn DMS into decimal degrees, add the degrees, the minutes divided by 60 and the seconds divided by 3600. North and east are positive; south and west are negative, so a latitude of 51 degrees 30 minutes north is positive and a longitude of 0 degrees 7 minutes west is negative.

Latitude runs from 0 to 90 in each hemisphere and longitude from 0 to 180. Minutes and seconds are always below 60. The converter checks every value you type and shows a plain message if a number is outside its range, instead of producing a wrong answer.

Worked example

51 degrees 30 minutes 26 seconds north becomes 51 + 30/60 + 26/3600 = 51 + 0.5 + 0.00722 = 51.50722 degrees. A longitude of 0 degrees 7 minutes 39 seconds west becomes 0 + 7/60 + 39/3600 = 0.1275 degrees, negative because it is west: -0.1275 degrees.

Going the other way, 51.50722 degrees gives 51 whole degrees, then 0.50722 x 60 = 30.4332 minutes, so 30 minutes, then 0.4332 x 60 = 25.99 seconds: 51 degrees 30 minutes 25.99 seconds north. The small difference from 26 seconds comes from rounding the decimal to five places.

Questions people ask

How do I convert DMS to decimal degrees?

Add degrees + minutes/60 + seconds/3600. South and west are negative, north and east are positive.

How do I convert decimal degrees back to DMS?

Take the whole degrees, multiply the remainder by 60 for minutes, then multiply the new remainder by 60 for seconds.

What do N, S, E and W mean?

N and E are positive, S and W are negative. Latitude runs 0 to 90 north or south; longitude runs 0 to 180 east or west.

Does the map link share my location?

The link only contains the numbers you typed. Nothing is sent anywhere until you click it.
