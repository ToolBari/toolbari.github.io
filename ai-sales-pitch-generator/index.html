How to use the pitch generator

Fill in the five boxes with your own details. The product, audience, problem and benefit boxes are required; the proof box is optional — if you leave it empty, the script offers a short trial instead of a claim. Then pick a length and press "Build pitch".

The tool shows the word count and an approximate speaking time for the finished script, so you can see whether it fits the slot you have. Copy the script and read it aloud at least once: any line that feels long when spoken should be trimmed, and any line that sounds like someone else should be rewritten in your own words.

The five parts of a pitch

Every script follows the same shape. The hook is a question that makes the listener picture life without the problem. The problem names the pain in the listener's own terms. The solution shows what your product does about it, in one line. The proof gives a reason to believe you — a result, a number, or a customer story. The call to action says what to do next, in one clear step.

The longer versions add detail to each part instead of adding new parts. A 60-second pitch has two lines per part; a 2-minute pitch has four. The shape stays the same, so the pitch still sounds like one story, not five separate ones.

How long is 30 seconds of talking?

At a natural speaking pace, most people say about 150 words a minute — so 30 seconds is about 75 words, 60 seconds about 150, and 2 minutes about 300. Those are rough guides, not exact counts: a nervous speaker goes faster, and a careful speaker goes slower.

That is why the tool shows the actual word count and an approximate speaking time for your script. If the time is longer than the slot you have, cut the middle lines first and keep the hook, the proof and the call to action — those are the parts the listener remembers.

Questions people ask

How long should my pitch be?

Match the setting. Thirty seconds fits a quick introduction or a short video; sixty seconds fits a meeting opener; two minutes fits a demo or a stall visit. When in doubt, pick the shorter version — a short pitch that lands beats a long one that wanders.

What if I have no proof yet?

Leave the proof box empty and the script offers a short trial instead of a claim. Never invent a result, a number or a customer story — one false claim can undo the whole pitch.

Can I change the wording?

Yes. Copy the script and edit it freely. The template gives you the shape; your own words, your own examples and your own voice are what make it yours.

Does the tool write the pitch for me?

It assembles your own details into template sentences in your browser. The ideas, the facts and the proof all come from you — the tool only arranges them into the five-part shape.
