Subscription Tracker

Add each subscription with its cost, billing cycle and renewal date, and the tracker adds up what you pay each month and each year. Upcoming renewals in the next 30 days are listed first, and your list is saved in this browser so it is still here next time.

Upcoming renewals (next 30 days)

Your list is stored only in this browser, on this device. Clearing browser data removes it, and it does not move to another device, so keep a separate copy of anything important.

How the totals are worked out

Every subscription is turned into a monthly figure before the totals are added up. A monthly cost is used as it is, a yearly cost is divided by 12, and a weekly cost is multiplied by 52 and divided by 12. The yearly total is simply the monthly total times 12.

Weekly costs are the trickiest, because a year has 52 weeks but only 12 months. Multiplying by 52 and dividing by 12 spreads the yearly cost of a weekly subscription evenly across the months, which keeps the monthly total honest.

Keeping subscriptions under control

Renewal dates are the moment to decide whether a subscription still earns its place. Check the upcoming renewals list once in a while and cancel anything you have not used since the last renewal. A subscription you forgot about still charges you, so a regular review is the simplest way to keep the total honest.

It also helps to write down why you keep each one. If the reason is vague, that is usually a sign the money could go somewhere better. The tracker shows the yearly total on purpose: a small monthly cost looks different when you see what it adds up to over a year.

Questions people ask

Where is my subscription list stored?

In this browser's local storage, on this device only. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing leaves your browser.

How are weekly and yearly costs turned into a monthly total?

A weekly cost is multiplied by 52 and divided by 12, and a yearly cost is divided by 12. Monthly costs are added as they are.

What counts as an upcoming renewal?

Any subscription whose renewal date falls within the next 30 days, counting from today. The list is sorted by the nearest renewal first.

Can I use the tracker without entering costs?

Costs are needed for the totals. If you only want renewal reminders, you can enter any cost and ignore the totals, since they are only for your own reference.
