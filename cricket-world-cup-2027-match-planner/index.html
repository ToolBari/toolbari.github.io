The tournament at a glance

The Cricket World Cup 2027 is hosted by three countries: South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia. It runs from Saturday 2 October to Sunday 21 November 2027, and it is a 50-over one-day international tournament. Fourteen teams take part, and 57 matches are played across 12 venues.

The three host nations share the matches between them: South Africa has eight venues, Zimbabwe has three, and Namibia has one. The tournament is long — seven weeks from the first match to the final — so planning which matches to watch matters.

The format is new for this tournament. It starts with a Super Series, moves into two groups of six, then a Super 7 stage, and finishes with two semi-finals and a final. The sections below walk through each stage, and the planner at the top of the page lets you follow one team through all of it.

The two groups

The group stage has 12 teams in two groups of six. Group A is India, Australia, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Zimbabwe and Qualifier A. Group B is New Zealand, South Africa, Sri Lanka, England, Bangladesh and Qualifier B. The two qualifier spots are filled by teams that come through the earlier stages of the tournament.

Every team plays the other five teams in its group once, which gives five matches per team and 15 matches per group. The two groups together play 30 group matches. The groups are fixed, so you can plan your watching around them with confidence: if you follow India, you know the five opponents are Australia, Pakistan, Afghanistan, Zimbabwe and Qualifier A.

How the format works

The tournament opens with the Super Series, in which the three lowest-ranked qualifiers play each other once in Namibia. The winner of that small round robin advances into the group stage, and the other two go home. Three matches are played in the Super Series.

Then comes the group stage: two groups of six, 15 matches per group, 30 matches in total. The top three teams in each group go through to the Super 7, and they are joined by the best fourth-placed team across the two groups. That makes seven teams in the Super 7, and they play each other once in a single round robin of 21 matches.

The top four teams in the Super 7 reach the semi-finals: Cape Town on 17 November and Centurion on 18 November. The two winners meet in the final on 21 November. Add it all up — 3 in the Super Series, 30 in the groups, 21 in the Super 7, 2 semi-finals and 1 final — and you get the 57 matches of the tournament.

The venues

Twelve venues host matches across the three host countries. South Africa has eight: Johannesburg, Tshwane and Centurion, Cape Town, Durban, Gqeberha, Bloemfontein, Paarl and KuGompo City. Zimbabwe has three: Harare, Bulawayo and Victoria Falls. Namibia has one: Windhoek.

The semi-finals are at two of the most famous grounds in South Africa: Newlands in Cape Town on 17 November, and SuperSport Park in Centurion on 18 November. The group stage and the Super 7 are spread across all twelve venues, so the tournament travels a long way across southern Africa.

If you are following one team, the venues matter less than the groups, because your team's five group matches could be in any of the venues in the host countries. If you are planning to travel, the venue list is the starting point for choosing where to be — and the semi-final venues are the two dates to protect first.

Key dates

The tournament opens on Saturday 2 October 2027 and the final is on Sunday 21 November 2027. Between those two dates there are seven weeks of cricket, and the only other dates this planner shows are the semi-finals: Wednesday 17 November in Cape Town and Thursday 18 November in Centurion.

Individual group matches are not listed by date here. This planner works from the groups, the rounds and the venues, and it does not present a fixture list. For the full fixture list with dates for every match, check the official ICC schedule, which is the only source that can give you each match's date.

That honesty is deliberate. A fixture list built from guesses would be worse than no fixture list at all, and a planner that pretends to know dates it does not know would send people to the wrong match on the wrong day. The dates on this page are the ones that are confirmed: the start, the semi-finals and the final.

Planning your watching

Seven weeks is a long tournament, so the best plan is a selective one. Start with your team's five group matches: those are the games that decide whether it reaches the Super 7, and they are the ones you will care about most. Then add the matches that decide the group — the games between the teams fighting for the top three places.

The Super 7 is where the tournament tightens. Seven teams play 21 matches, and every result matters, because only four teams reach the semi-finals. If you followed the group stage loosely, the Super 7 is the stage to follow closely: it is where the final four take shape.

Finally, protect the last week. The semi-finals on 17 and 18 November and the final on 21 November are the three matches the whole tournament builds toward. Whatever else you watch, those three days are the ones to keep free.
