Attendance Percentage Calculator

Enter the classes you have attended and the total classes held to see your attendance percentage. The calculator also works out how many more classes you can miss, or how many you must attend in a row, to reach the target you set.

This is arithmetic on the numbers you enter. Your school or college may count attendance differently, for example by subject or by term, so use the official record for anything formal.

How the percentage is worked out

Attendance percentage is simply the classes you attended divided by the classes held, multiplied by 100. If you attended 36 of 40 classes, that is 36 divided by 40 times 100, which is 90 percent. The calculator shows the working next to the result so you can check it at a glance.

The "classes you can miss" figure comes from the same idea in reverse. It finds the largest number of extra classes you could skip while your attended count stays at or above your target share of the new total. For example, with 36 attended out of 40 and a target of 75 percent, you can miss 8 more classes, because 36 out of 48 is exactly 75 percent.

Reaching a target from below

If your attendance is under your target, the calculator works out how many classes in a row you must attend to climb back to it. With 20 attended out of 30 and a target of 75 percent, you need 10 classes in a row, because 30 out of 40 is 75 percent. Each class you attend moves the percentage up, but the climb gets slower as the total grows, so the sooner you start the fewer classes it takes.

One special case is a target of 100 percent. If you have already missed a class, no number of future classes can bring you back to a perfect record, because the missed class stays in the total. The calculator says so plainly instead of giving a misleading number.

Questions people ask

What if I have attended every class so far?

Then your attendance is 100 percent. Enter the same number for attended and total, and the calculator will also tell you how many classes you can miss before dropping below your target.

Can the target be 100 percent?

Yes. If you have a perfect record you are already there, but if you have missed even one class the calculator will tell you that 100 percent is no longer reachable.

Do I count online classes and labs?

Count whatever your school or college counts as a class. If labs and online sessions are recorded separately, run the calculator once for each group.

What about a class I attended for only part of the time?

That depends on your institution's rules. Some count a partial attendance as present and some do not, so check the official record and enter the numbers as they stand.
