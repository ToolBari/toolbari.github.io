What goes on a delivery note

A delivery note travels with the goods and says what is in the delivery. It names the sender and the recipient, gives the delivery address and date, carries a reference, and lists every item with its quantity. The recipient checks the goods against the note when they arrive.

The signature line at the bottom is there for a reason: a signed delivery note is proof the goods arrived. Keep a copy of every note you send, and ask the recipient to note any shortage on the note itself before signing.

Using the note with a delivery

Print the note and put one copy inside the parcel and one with the driver or courier if you use one. If the delivery is made in person, hand the note over with the goods and ask for the signature there and then, while any problem can still be sorted out on the spot.

Delivery notes work best alongside a purchase order or an invoice, not instead of one. The order says what was agreed, the note says what was sent, and the invoice says what is owed, so each document has its own job.

Questions people ask

What is a delivery note for?

A delivery note travels with goods and lists what is in the delivery, who sent it and who should receive it. The recipient can check the goods against the note when they arrive.

Does a delivery note show prices?

No. This maker lists items and quantities only, because a delivery note is a record of goods sent, not a bill.

What if an item is missing on arrival?

The recipient should note the shortage on the delivery note and contact the sender. This page cannot track deliveries or resolve shortages.

Is the delivery note saved anywhere?

No. The note exists only in this page until you print or download it, so save a copy before you leave.
