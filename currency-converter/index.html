Questions people ask

Are the exchange rates live?

The converter uses a clearly dated rate snapshot (21 October 2026, from ExchangeRate-API open data) and shows the date on the tool. For a quick check that is plenty — but cash rates at banks, bKash and money exchanges usually differ slightly, so always check the rate there before sending money.

Which currencies can I convert?

29 currencies, including Bangladeshi Taka (BDT), US Dollar (USD), Saudi Riyal (SAR), UAE Dirham, Euro (EUR) and British Pound (GBP).

Why does the bank or bKash give me a different amount?

Banks and mobile-money services use their own cash rates and may add charges, so the amount that finally arrives can differ a little from any converter estimate.
