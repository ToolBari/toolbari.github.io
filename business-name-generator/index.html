Questions people ask

How does the Business Name Generator work?

Type one keyword — your product, craft or area, like cake — and the generator builds 8 name ideas from proven patterns: your keyword with Hub, Lab, Studio, Point or Bari, plus Sweet, My and The … House forms. The keyword's first letter is capitalised automatically.

How many name ideas do I get?

Eight ideas per keyword, instantly. Try a second keyword — a place name, your own name, or a shorter word — to get eight more and mix the two lists.

Can I just use one of these names for my real business?

The names are starting ideas, not a clearance check. Before you print a signboard or open a page, search the name on Facebook and Google and check the .com domain, so you are not copying an existing business.
