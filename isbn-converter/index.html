ISBN Converter

Paste an ISBN-10 or ISBN-13 and this tool validates its check digit, then converts it to the other format with a freshly computed check digit. Numbers that fail validation are flagged instead of converted.

Only ISBN-13 numbers starting with 978 can be converted to ISBN-10. An ISBN-13 starting with 979 has no ISBN-10 equivalent, so the tool will say so.

How to use the converter

Paste an ISBN-10 or ISBN-13 into the box, with or without hyphens, and press Convert. The tool first checks that the number is valid, then shows the converted number in the other format, together with the check-digit working.

If the number fails validation, the tool says so and does not convert it. That usually means a mistyped digit, so check the number against the book and try again.

How ISBN check digits work

An ISBN-10 has 10 characters. Its check digit is found by weighting the first nine digits from 10 down to 2, adding them up, and choosing the last character so the total divides evenly by 11. A check value of 10 is written as the letter X.

An ISBN-13 has 13 digits. Its check digit weights the first twelve digits alternately by 1 and 3, and is chosen so the full total is a multiple of 10. Converting to ISBN-13 adds the 978 prefix and recomputes the check digit.

Where to find a book's ISBN

The ISBN is printed above the barcode on the back cover of most books, and on the copyright page near the front. Older books may show an ISBN-10, while newer books show an ISBN-13.

When a book lists both formats, either one works with this tool, and converting one should give you the other. If the two do not match, one of them was printed or typed with an error. A book with several editions has a different ISBN for each edition, so always use the number from the copy in front of you.

Questions people ask

Can I paste an ISBN with hyphens?

Yes. Hyphens and spaces are ignored, so you can paste the number exactly as printed on the book.

What does the letter X mean in an ISBN-10?

X stands for the value 10 as a check digit. It can only appear as the last character of an ISBN-10.

Why can some ISBN-13 numbers not become ISBN-10?

ISBN-10 only covers numbers that begin with the 978 prefix. ISBN-13 numbers beginning with 979 have no ISBN-10 form.

What if my ISBN fails validation?

The check digit does not match, which usually means a mistyped digit. Check the number against the book and try again.
