AI API Cost Calculator

Enter your tokens per request, your prices per million tokens, and how many requests you make each day. The calculator shows the cost per request, per day, and per month, with the working shown.

This tool works fully in your browser and calls no AI service, API, or backend. It only multiplies the figures you enter, so the result is only as accurate as your token counts and prices. Check your provider's current pricing before budgeting.

How the cost is worked out

Providers usually charge per million tokens, with one price for input tokens and another for output tokens. The calculator multiplies your input tokens by the input price and your output tokens by the output price, then adds the two together to get the cost of one request. It then multiplies by your requests per day for the daily cost, and by 30 days for the monthly figure. Every step is shown, so you can check the arithmetic yourself.

A worked example

Suppose each request uses 1,000 input tokens and 500 output tokens, your input price is 2 per million tokens, your output price is 8 per million tokens, and you make 100 requests a day. Input cost is 1,000 divided by 1,000,000 times 2, which is 0.002. Output cost is 500 divided by 1,000,000 times 8, which is 0.004. One request costs 0.006, one day costs 0.6, and one month costs 18. Enter these same figures in the calculator to see it work them out.

What counts as input and output tokens

Input tokens cover everything you send: your prompt, any instructions, and any text you include for the model to work with. Output tokens cover everything the model sends back. Long prompts, repeated instructions, and long answers all raise the total. If you are unsure how many tokens a text is worth, measure it with a token counter first, then enter the figures here. Estimates are fine for planning, but real usage will vary from request to request.

Reading the monthly figure

The monthly figure assumes 30 days at the same daily rate, which is a planning figure rather than a bill. Real months have different lengths, usage goes up and down, and some providers charge for extras such as storage or fine-tuning, which this calculator does not cover. Treat the result as a budget guide: if the monthly figure surprises you, look at which part drives it, usually the output tokens or the number of requests, and plan around that.

Questions people ask

Where do I find my prices?

On your provider's pricing page, listed as a price per million tokens for input and output. Enter those figures exactly as shown; this calculator has no preset prices.

Why is a month 30 days here?

Thirty days is a simple planning figure. Real months vary, so treat the monthly result as a budget guide rather than an exact bill.

Does it include taxes or fees?

No. It only multiplies the token figures and prices you enter. Taxes, discounts, and extra charges are not included.

What currency does it use?

Whatever label you type in the currency field. The calculator never assumes a currency, so enter your own label or leave it blank.
