Birthday Video Maker

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Make a birthday slideshow video from your photos and a birthday message in your browser, then download it.





Your photos stay in your browser and are never uploaded. The video downloads as WebM (not MP4) — it plays in browsers and most players, but some apps may need conversion. No music is added. Recording needs a browser with MediaRecorder support.

How to use

  1. Choose your birthday photos.
  2. Type the birthday person's name and message.
  3. Pick how long each photo shows.
  4. Tap Record Video, then download it.

Example: Choose 3 photos, type the name Ayesha, and record a birthday slideshow video.
