AI Interview Practice

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Practice job interviews with AI free in your browser — pick a role, answer questions, and get simple feedback.



Questions and feedback are generated by a free third-party AI service (Pollinations). It can be slow or fail at times — if it fails, the tool shows an error. This is practice only, not a real interview or hiring decision.

How to use

  1. Type the job role you want.
  2. Tap Generate Questions.
  3. Answer one question out loud or in writing.
  4. Tap Get Feedback for simple AI feedback.

Example: Type "shop assistant", get interview questions, and practise your answers.
