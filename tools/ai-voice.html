AI Voice Generator

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Turn text into spoken voice with your browser's built-in speech — pick a voice, speed and pitch, then listen.





This tool uses your browser's built-in speech synthesis — it is not an AI voice model, and the voices available depend on your device and browser. No audio file is created.

How to use

  1. Type or paste your text.
  2. Pick a voice, speed and pitch.
  3. Tap Speak to listen.
  4. Tap Stop to end playback.

Example: Type "Hello, welcome to ToolBari", pick a voice, and tap Speak.
