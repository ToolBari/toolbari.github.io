Easy Video Maker

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Make an easy slideshow video from your photos and text in your browser — add slides, preview, then download it.




Your photos stay in your browser and are never uploaded. This is a general slideshow maker — for a birthday theme, use the Birthday Video Maker. The video downloads as WebM (not MP4) and needs a browser with MediaRecorder support. No music is added.

How to use

  1. Choose your photos in slide order.
  2. Type one caption per line, matching the photos.
  3. Pick how long each slide shows.
  4. Tap Record Video, then download it.

Example: Choose 3 photos, type 3 caption lines, and record a general slideshow video.
