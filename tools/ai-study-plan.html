AI Study Plan Maker

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Make a study plan with AI free in your browser — add your subject, days and daily hours, then follow the plan.




Plans are generated by a free third-party AI service (Pollinations). It can be slow or fail at times — if it fails, the tool shows an error. The Template Version is a simple day-by-day split \u2014 template-based, not real AI. Take breaks and adjust the plan to your own pace.

How to use

  1. Type your subject or exam.
  2. Set how many days you have and hours per day.
  3. Tap Make Study Plan.
  4. Follow the day-by-day plan.

Example: Type "English exam", 7 days, 2 hours a day, and get a weekly plan.
