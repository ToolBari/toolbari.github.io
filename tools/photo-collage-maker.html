Photo Collage Maker

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Combine your photos into one collage in your browser — pick a layout, add photos, then download it as PNG.



Your photos stay in your browser and are never uploaded. Empty slots stay light grey if you add fewer photos than the layout needs.

How to use

  1. Choose up to 4 photos.
  2. Pick a collage layout.
  3. Tap Make Collage to preview it.
  4. Tap Download PNG to save it.

Example: Choose 4 photos, pick the 2 × 2 grid, and download one collage image.
