AI Story & Script Writer

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Write a short AI story or video script from your idea — choose story or script, set a length, and generate.




Writing is generated by a free third-party AI service (Pollinations), not by templates. If the service is down, the tool shows an error instead of fake writing.

How to use

  1. Type your story or script idea.
  2. Choose Story or Script and a length.
  3. Tap Generate and wait for the writing.
  4. Copy the text you like.

Example: Type "a lost cat finds its way home", choose Story and Short, and read the result.
