Voice to Text Note

Uses your browser's built-in speech recognition — not an AI voice model.

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Speak and turn your voice into a written note in your browser — copy, save or download it as a text file.



Voice recognition uses your browser's built-in speech service and works best in Chrome on desktop or Android. It needs microphone permission and an internet connection in Chrome, and it may mishear words — always check the note before saving it.

How to use

  1. Tap Start Listening and allow microphone access.
  2. Speak clearly — your words appear as text.
  3. Tap Stop, then edit the note if needed.
  4. Copy the note or download it as a text file.

Example: Tap Start Listening and say "Buy milk and eggs tomorrow" to see it written out.
