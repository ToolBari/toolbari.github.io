AI Email Writer

Last updated: 27 October 2026 — ToolBari

Write a professional email with AI free in your browser — pick a purpose, tone and recipient, then copy it.





Emails are generated by a free third-party AI service (Pollinations). It can be slow or fail at times — if it fails, the tool shows an error. The Template Version is template-based only, not real AI. Always check an email before sending it.

How to use

  1. Pick an email purpose and tone.
  2. Type the recipient and the key points.
  3. Tap Generate Email and wait for the AI draft.
  4. Copy or edit the email before sending it.

Example: Pick Thank-you, type "thanks for the interview", and get a polite draft.
