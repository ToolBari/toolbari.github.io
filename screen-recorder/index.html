The real requirements — exactly as the tool itself states them

  • Browser: it works best on desktop Chrome or Edge, which support the browser's built-in screen-capture feature.
  • Phones: many mobile browsers do not support screen recording. On those, the tool shows a clear "not supported" message instead of failing silently.
  • Your choice, every time: the browser — not the page — asks you to pick the screen, window or tab, and you can cancel. Nothing records without that step.
  • Format: the finished recording is a WebM video file, saved as screen-recording.webm.

Good uses

  • Recording a quick tutorial or showing someone a problem on your screen.
  • Saving an online class, meeting or demo shown in a browser tab.
  • Capturing a bug or an error exactly as it happens, to send to support.

Questions people ask

What do I need to use this screen recorder?

A desktop browser with screen-capture support — it works best on desktop Chrome or Edge. When you press Start, the browser asks you to choose which screen, window or tab to record; nothing is recorded until you choose and allow it.

Does it work on a phone?

Mostly no. Many mobile browsers do not support screen recording at all. On an unsupported device or browser the tool does not pretend — it shows a message saying screen recording is not supported and to try desktop Chrome or Edge.

What format is the recorded video?

WebM. When you stop, the recording appears as a preview on the page and the download saves as screen-recording.webm. The recorder asks the browser for audio as well as video, but whether sound is included depends on what you share and your browser's options.

Is my recording uploaded anywhere?

No. Recording happens entirely in your browser on your device; the video is created locally and downloaded straight to your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

How do I stop recording?

Press the Stop button, or use the browser's own Stop sharing control. Either way the recording finishes and the preview and download appear on the page.
