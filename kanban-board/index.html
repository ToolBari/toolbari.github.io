How to use the board

Type a task, choose the column it belongs in, and press Add task. The card appears in that column with the number of cards in each column shown at the top. To move a card, drag it to another column, or use the Left and Right buttons on the card itself. The buttons are the easiest way on a phone, where dragging can be fiddly. Press Delete on a card to remove it, or Clear board to remove every card at once.

Every change is saved straight away in your browser, so you can close the tab and pick up where you left off. There is no account and nothing is sent anywhere: the board belongs to this browser on this device. If your browser blocks storage, the board still works for the session, and a message will tell you that saving is unavailable.

A simple way to work through tasks

A kanban board works best when the columns show the real state of your work. Put new ideas and jobs in To Do, move a task to Doing only when you actually start it, and move it to Done the moment it is finished. Seeing the Doing column stay short is the whole point: it keeps your attention on the few things in progress instead of the long list of things still to do.

Try reviewing the board once a day. Move finished work across, delete cards that no longer matter, and add anything new that came up. A board that is looked at every day stays honest, and a board that stays honest is one you will keep using. For bigger jobs, break them into smaller cards so each move across the board feels like real progress.

Questions people ask

Where is my board saved?

In your browser local storage on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and the board loads again when you reopen the page.

Can I move a card without dragging?

Yes. Every card has Left and Right buttons that move it to the next column, which is handy on a phone.

How do I delete a card?

Press the Delete button on the card. Deleted cards cannot be brought back.

How many cards can I add?

There is no set limit. The board stays fast with a normal number of cards, and you can clear the whole board any time.
