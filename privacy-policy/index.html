Privacy Policy

ToolBari is a collection of free browser-based tools, and this page explains what data is — and is not — collected when you use it. Almost every tool runs entirely in your browser, so the files and text you use are never uploaded to our servers.

Last updated: November 2026

ToolBari — All In One Tools ("we", "this site") is a collection of free browser-based utilities. We respect your privacy. This page explains what data is — and is not — collected when you use this site. In short: most tools run entirely in your browser, a small number use third-party services to work, and we do not build personal profiles or run accounts.

Files you process

Almost every tool on this site — image tools, the PDF maker, calculators, text tools, the QR generator and others — runs entirely inside your own browser. The images, documents and text you use with these tools are never uploaded to or stored on our servers. We do not even operate a file server for them, so there is nowhere for your files to go. When you close the page, your work stays on your own device and nowhere else.

Tools that use third-party services

A small number of tools send the specific link you paste to a third-party public service in order to function. The Video Downloader uses a relay service we operate, which forwards the pasted link to a download backend. The TikTok Downloader uses TikWM, the Short Link Generator uses spoo.me, the Breaking News tool reads news RSS feeds via AllOrigins, the Stock Exchange tool uses TradingView, and the Instagram DP Viewer uses Unavatar. The Currency Converter fetches public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API, Prayer Times uses the Aladhan API, Weather uses Open-Meteo, the IP & Device tool uses public IP-information services, and the DNS Checker queries Google Public DNS.

Only the URL or username you enter is sent, and only when you press the button. Nothing is sent while you type. Those services have their own privacy policies, and what they do with the data they receive is covered by their policies, not this one.

Browser storage

Your starred "favorite tools" are saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your device. That list never leaves your device, and no account is created and no personal profile is built. Clearing your browser data will remove your favorites, because they live only in that browser. If you use ToolBari in a different browser or on a different device, your favorites will not follow you — they are stored per browser, not per person.

Advertising

No third-party advertisements are currently displayed on this site. Google AdSense code is present on some pages and is under review by Google; no ads are served at this time. If advertising begins in the future, this section will be updated to name the provider and describe any cookies it sets. You can control cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Children

This site is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The tools are open to everyone and ask for no personal details, so there is nothing for a child to hand over by using them. If you are a parent and you have a question about a tool, see the Contact page.

Questions about this policy? See the Contact page.

Questions people ask

Do my files get uploaded?

No. Almost every tool on ToolBari runs entirely in your browser, so the images, documents and text you use are never uploaded to or stored on our servers. We do not even operate a file server for them.

Which tools send data to other services?

A small number: the Video Downloader, TikTok Downloader, Short Link Generator, Breaking News tool, Stock Exchange tool and Instagram DP Viewer send the link or username you enter to a third-party service, and the Currency Converter, Prayer Times, Weather, IP & Device and DNS Checker tools fetch public data from their providers. Only the URL or username you enter is sent, and only when you press the button.

Do you use cookies?

The tools themselves do not set cookies: your starred favorite tools are saved in your browser's local storage, not in a cookie. The site uses Google Analytics to count visitors, which may set its own cookies. No ads are served at this time. You can control cookies in your browser settings at any time.

Are there ads on ToolBari?

No. No third-party advertisements are currently displayed on this site. Google AdSense code is present on some pages and is under review by Google, but no ads are served at this time. If advertising begins in the future, this section of the policy will be updated to name the provider and describe any cookies it sets.

Do you collect data from children?

No. ToolBari is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The tools ask for no personal details, and no account is created.
