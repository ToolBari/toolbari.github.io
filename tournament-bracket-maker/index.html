Tournament Bracket Maker

Paste your team or player names and get a single-elimination bracket with every round laid out. Click a winner to move them to the next round, and print the bracket when it is ready.

The bracket lives only on this page. It is not saved, so print it or keep the tab open if you need it later.

How to build your bracket

Type or paste the names, one per line, in the order you want them paired. The first two names meet in the first match, the next two in the second, and so on. You can enter from 2 to 32 teams or players, and each name must be different so the results stay clear. Empty lines are ignored, so a stray blank line will not break anything.

If the number of teams is not a power of two, the bracket is filled to the next size and the extra slots become byes. A team drawn against a bye moves to the next round automatically, and the bracket marks those matches so everyone can see why. With 5 teams, for example, three teams get a first-round bye and the other two play to join them.

Running the tournament

When a match is played, click the winning team in that match. The winner moves into their next-round match straight away, and the button turns green so the result is easy to spot. If you click the wrong team, just click the other one: the result changes, and any later rounds that depended on it are cleared so the bracket stays consistent.

Keep going until the final is decided, and the champion is shown at the top of the bracket. Print the bracket at any point to pin it up or hand it out. Because the bracket is not saved, print it before you close the page if you need a record of the results.

Questions people ask

How many teams can I enter?

From 2 to 32 teams or players, one name per line.

What happens if I have an odd number of teams?

The bracket is filled to the next power of two, and the extra slots become byes, so some teams skip the first round.

Can I change a result?

Yes. Click the other team in a match to change the winner, and the later rounds update.

How do I print the bracket?

Press Print to open your browser print dialog. The bracket prints in rounds from left to right.
