Present Value Calculator

Enter a future value, a discount rate per period and the number of periods to find its present value. The calculator also shows the discount factor and how much value the discount removes.

This is arithmetic on the numbers you enter. It does not predict real prices or interest rates, and it is not financial advice.

The present value formula

Present value answers a simple question: what is a future amount worth today, if money grows at a given rate per period? The formula divides the future value by one plus the rate, raised to the number of periods. The result is the amount that would grow into the future value over that time at that rate.

As a worked example, a future value of 1000 with a rate of 5 percent per period over 3 periods has a present value of 863.84. The discount factor is 1 divided by 1.05 to the power of 3, which is 0.8638, and 1000 times 0.8638 is 863.84. The discount removes 136.16 of value.

Reading the discount breakdown

The breakdown shows three things: the discount factor, the present value and the total discount. The factor is the share of the future value that survives the discount, so a factor of 0.86 means about 86 percent of the value remains. The table below the result shows the value at each period, starting at the present value and growing period by period until it reaches the future value.

A period can be any regular interval: a year, a month or a quarter. The only rule is that the rate and the periods must use the same interval, so a yearly rate goes with a number of years and a monthly rate with a number of months. A monthly rate with 36 periods means three years of monthly discounting, while the same rate with 3 periods means only three months.

Questions people ask

What happens if the rate is zero?

The present value equals the future value, because no discount is applied. The discount factor is 1 and the total discount is zero.

What is a period in this calculator?

A period is one regular interval of time, such as a year or a month. Use the same interval for the rate and the number of periods.

Can the rate be a fraction like 2.5 percent?

Yes. Enter the rate with decimals as needed, for example 2.5 for two and a half percent per period.

What is the difference between present value and future value?

Present value is what an amount is worth today, and future value is what it becomes later at the given rate. This calculator works backwards from the future value to today.
