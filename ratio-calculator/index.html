How ratios work

A ratio compares two amounts. The ratio 12 : 18 says that for every 12 units of the first amount there are 18 units of the second. Ratios are usually written in their simplest form, where the two numbers share no common factor other than 1. To simplify a ratio, divide both parts by their greatest common divisor. The greatest common divisor of 12 and 18 is 6, so 12 : 18 simplifies to 2 : 3. Both forms describe the same relationship, but the simple form is easier to read and to scale up or down.

A ratio can also be read as fractions of a whole. In the ratio 2 : 3 there are 2 + 3 = 5 equal parts, so the first amount is 2/5 of the whole and the second is 3/5. This is handy when you split something in a given ratio, for example sharing an amount between two people in the ratio 2 : 3. The calculator shows these fractions next to every simplified ratio.

Solving a proportion

A proportion states that two ratios are equal, for example 3 : 4 = 9 : x. To find the missing value, cross-multiply: 3 times x equals 4 times 9, so x = 36 / 3 = 12. The same idea scales recipes, maps and mixing instructions. If a paint mix needs 3 parts colour to 4 parts base, and you have 9 parts of colour, you need 12 parts of base. The proportion section of the calculator does this working for you and shows each step.

Questions people ask

How do I simplify a ratio?

Divide both parts of the ratio by their greatest common divisor. For 12 : 18 the greatest common divisor is 6, so dividing both parts by 6 gives 2 : 3.

What is a proportion?

A proportion is a statement that two ratios are equal, such as 3 : 4 = 9 : 12. If one value is missing, cross-multiplication finds it: multiply the two known values on opposite sides and divide by the remaining known value.

How do I turn a ratio into a fraction?

Add the parts of the ratio to get the total number of parts. In the ratio 2 : 3 there are 5 parts, so the first part is 2/5 of the whole and the second part is 3/5.

Can a ratio include decimals?

Yes. This calculator accepts decimal values and scales them to whole numbers before simplifying, so 1.5 : 2.25 simplifies to 2 : 3, the same as 150 : 225.
