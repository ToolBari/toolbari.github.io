How to use the teleprompter

Paste your script into the box and press Load script. The text appears in the dark prompter window below, and the window scrolls from the top when you press Play. The speed control runs from 1, the slowest, to 10, the fastest, and you can change it while the text is scrolling, so you can slow down for a tricky passage or speed up through a part you know well.

Pause stops the scrolling exactly where it is, and Play starts it again. Back to top returns to the beginning of the script. The font size control changes the text in the window straight away, with four sizes from small to extra large. Larger text is easier to read from a distance, while smaller text fits more of the script on screen at once.

Reading comfortably

Before you record, do a practice run at the speed you plan to use. Reading aloud is slower than reading in your head, so a speed that looks right on a first glance can feel rushed once you start speaking. If you keep catching up with the text, drop the speed by one and try again. Short paragraphs and blank lines between sections make the script easier to follow while it moves.

The mirror option flips the text left to right for use with a reflector screen in front of a camera. In that setup the camera reads through the glass while you read the reflection, so the mirrored text appears the right way round to you. Leave the option off when you are reading on a plain screen, a tablet or a laptop.

Questions people ask

How do I load my script?

Paste your text in the script box and press Load script. The text appears in the prompter window below.

Can I change the speed while it scrolls?

Yes. The speed control takes effect straight away, so you can slow down or speed up mid-read.

What is the mirror option for?

Mirror flips the text left to right for use with a reflector screen in front of a camera. Leave it off for reading on a plain screen.

Is my script saved anywhere?

No. The script stays in this browser tab only and is gone when you close the page.
