How to Resize a Photo for a Passport or Online Form

Do it in this order: 1) copy the exact size from the application notice; 2) open the matching tool below; 3) position the whole face or signature inside the frame before resizing; 4) download; 5) check the finished file's pixel dimensions — and if only the KB is too high, compress it without changing the pixels.

Questions people ask

What size is a Bangladesh passport photo on ToolBari?

The ToolBari Passport Photo Maker makes a 40×50 mm photo on a 472×590 pixel canvas and can also make an eight-copy print sheet. If an application notice gives a different size, the notice is final.

What photo and signature sizes do Bangladesh job forms commonly use?

The ToolBari Job Photo & Signature Sizer uses 300×300 pixels for the photo and 300×80 pixels for the signature, following the general sizes used by most Bangladesh government job and admission forms. Always follow the size in your own notice if it differs.

Why does resizing crop the sides or top of my photo?

Cropping happens when the original photo's width-to-height ratio differs from the target ratio and the tool fills the whole frame. The checker on this page shows the percentage that would be cropped before you resize.

How can I avoid cutting off a face or signature?

Position the face or signature fully inside the frame with the tool's zoom and position controls before downloading, leave a small margin around it, and check the preview and the final pixel dimensions.

What if the pixels are right but the file is too many KB?

Keep the pixel dimensions and compress the JPG instead. Lowering the JPG quality reduces KB without changing the width and height the form asked for.
