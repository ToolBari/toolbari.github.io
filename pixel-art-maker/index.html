Pixel Art Maker

Draw pixel art right in your browser. Pick a grid size and a colour, paint cells by clicking or dragging, and download your finished art as a PNG image.

Your art lives only on this page while it is open. Download the PNG to keep it, because refreshing the page clears the grid.

How to draw

Pick a colour from the palette, then click a cell to paint it, or hold the mouse button down and drag to paint many cells in one stroke. The eraser paints cells back to blank, and the Clear button empties the whole grid at once.

Changing the grid size starts a fresh grid, so download anything you want to keep before switching sizes. The 16 by 16 grid suits simple icons, while the 64 by 64 grid gives room for detail.

Ideas to try

Start with something small: a heart, a smiley face, a mushroom, or the first letter of your name. Simple shapes with two or three colours look best at small grid sizes.

Once you are comfortable, try a game character, a fruit, or a repeating pattern. Working from a reference picture open beside the page helps with trickier shapes.

Saving and sharing your art

The Download button saves your art as a PNG image, 512 by 512 pixels, ready to share anywhere images are accepted. The white background keeps the art looking clean on a light page, and the square shape suits profile pictures and social posts.

If you want a bigger image, open the PNG in any image editor and scale it up with the nearest-neighbour setting, which keeps the crisp pixel edges instead of blurring them. Download before refreshing the page, because the grid does not survive a reload.

Questions people ask

What grid sizes are there?

Three sizes: 16 by 16, 32 by 32 and 64 by 64 cells. Smaller grids suit simple icons, while larger grids give room for detail.

How do I erase?

Press the Eraser button, then click or drag over the cells you want to clear. Picking any colour turns the eraser off again.

What format is the download?

The download is a PNG image, 512 by 512 pixels, with your painted cells on a white background.

Is my art saved?

No. Your art exists only on this page while it is open. Download the PNG to keep a copy, because refreshing the page clears the grid.
