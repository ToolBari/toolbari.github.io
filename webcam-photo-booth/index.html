Webcam Photo Booth

The webcam photo booth turns your camera into a photo booth in your browser. Pick a filter and a frame, use the countdown, take a photo, and download it as a PNG — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Press Start camera to begin.
Your browser will ask for camera permission before the preview starts. Photos are made on your device and are never uploaded, but anyone who can see your screen can see the camera preview.

How to take a photo

Press Start camera and allow the permission your browser asks for. When the preview appears, pick a filter and a frame, then choose a countdown if you want time to pose. Press Take photo and the picture appears below the preview, ready to download as a PNG file.

The countdown counts down in large numbers over the preview, then captures the photo. If you take another photo, the new one replaces the download, and the gallery below keeps small copies of your last six photos so you can see the whole session at a glance.

Filters and frames

Filters change the look of the photo: black and white for a classic feel, sepia for a warm old-photo look, invert for a negative effect, soft blur for a dreamy feel, and high contrast for bolder colours. The preview shows the filter live, so what you see is what the photo will look like.

Frames change the shape of the saved photo. The polaroid border adds a white edge with a wider strip at the bottom, rounded corners softens the edges, and the circle frame crops the photo into a round picture. Frames are applied to the saved photo, not just the preview.

Questions people ask

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The camera stream, the photo and the download all happen in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent to any server.

Why does the camera not start?

The most common reasons are a denied permission, a camera already in use by another app, or a browser that does not support camera access. Check your browser permission settings and make sure no other app is using the camera.

What format are the photos?

Photos are downloaded as PNG image files, named photo-booth.png. The PNG keeps the full quality of the captured frame.

Can I take more than one photo?

Yes. Take as many photos as you like. The gallery keeps small copies of your last six photos, and the download button always saves the most recent one.
