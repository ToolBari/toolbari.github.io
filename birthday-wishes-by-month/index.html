How the month pages work

Each month card on this page opens a gallery for that month, with one photo for every date. Open the month, find the day, and you will see the photo made for that date — each one has its own design, so no two dates look alike. The photos are sized for phones, so they look right when you share them in a message or post them on a feed. There is nothing to install and no account to make: pick a month, pick a date, and the photo is ready.

Picking the right photo

The simplest way to pick is to think about the person. A bright, playful design suits a child or a close friend, while a calm, elegant one suits a parent or an elder. If you are not sure, look through the month page and pick the design that feels right — there is no wrong choice, because every photo carries its own warm wish. For a special touch, you can pair the photo with a card of your own: the birthday card maker lets you add your own words to a card to go with the photo.

Sharing tips

Download the photo first, then share it from your own phone — that way it arrives in full quality instead of as a small preview. Send it on the morning of the birthday so the person wakes up to it, and add a line of your own: a name, a memory, or a short wish makes a ready-made photo feel personal. If you want to dress the photo up further, the birthday frames collection offers frames you can add a photo to. And if the birthday falls near the end of the year, the December birthday wishes page has a photo for every date in December, including the holiday season.
