What makes a clear offer poster

A good offer poster answers three questions at a glance: what is on offer, what it costs now, and how long the offer lasts. The product name should be short and specific, the new price should be the biggest number on the poster, and the old price should sit beside it so the saving is obvious.

Always include the end date of the offer. A poster without a validity date leaves shoppers guessing, and an expired offer shared by mistake causes arguments at the counter.

Sharing your poster

The poster is a square image, 1080 by 1080 pixels, which suits social media posts and messaging apps. Download the PNG to share it online, or use the Print button for a copy to put in the shop window.

Everything is drawn in your browser. Your shop details and prices are never uploaded anywhere, so the poster is private until you choose to share it.

Keeping offers honest

A poster is a promise, so only advertise what you can actually sell at the price shown. If stock is limited, say so on the poster rather than turning shoppers away at the counter. If the offer ends on a certain date, make sure the date on the poster matches the date you will honour.

When an offer changes, make a fresh poster instead of editing an old image. A new poster avoids confusion and keeps the details accurate for everyone who sees it.

Questions people ask

How is the discount worked out?

If you enter both the old and new price, the discount percent is worked out from them automatically. You can also type a percent yourself, which takes priority over the automatic figure.

What size is the poster?

The poster is 1080 by 1080 pixels, a square image that suits social media posts, messaging apps and printing at a small size.

Can I print the poster?

Yes. Use the Print button after making the poster, or download the PNG and print it from your photo viewer or print shop.

Is my shop information uploaded?

No. The poster is drawn in your browser from what you type, and nothing leaves your device unless you share the downloaded image yourself.
