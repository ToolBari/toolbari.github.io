How to share these birthday photos

Find the photo for the birthday date and save it to your device — long-press the photo on a phone, or right-click it on a computer and choose save. Then send it as a message or post it on WhatsApp, Facebook, or Messenger. The photos are free to share for personal use, so you can send one to as many July birthdays as you like.

All of them. The gallery has one birthday photo for each day from 1 July to 31 July, so every July birthday is covered.

Can I share a photo on WhatsApp or Facebook?

Yes. Download the photo for the birthday date, then share it from your phone or computer on WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, or any app you use.
