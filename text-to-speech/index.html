Voices: the honest, exact answer

There is no single ToolBari voice catalogue. The tool asks your browser for speechSynthesis.getVoices() — the voices installed or supplied on that phone or computer — and lists each one by its real name and language. A phone with a Bengali voice shows it; a device without one cannot invent it. When a Bengali voice exists, it is preselected.

Use it for

  • Listening to study notes, messages and web text instead of reading a small screen.
  • Checking how English or Bengali text sounds before recording or presenting it.
  • Reading aloud at 0.5× for learning, or up to 1.8× to scan familiar text quickly.

Voice Typing is in the same full tool

The full ToolBari view pairs this reader with speech-to-text in Bengali (Bangladesh), English (US) and English (UK). Dictation works best in Chrome and asks for microphone permission.

Questions people ask

What languages can Text to Speech read?

It can read any language for which your browser or device provides a voice. ToolBari loads the actual voice list from your browser, so the names and languages in the dropdown are the real choices on your device — there is no fixed universal list.

Which voice is selected first?

If a Bengali voice is available, ToolBari selects it first. Otherwise it uses the first available voice, and you can change the voice in the dropdown.

Can I change the speaking speed?

Yes. The speed control runs from 0.5× to 1.8×, in 0.1 steps. The default is 1×.

Does Text to Speech download an audio file?

No. The ToolBari tool speaks the text aloud live and has Play and Stop controls; it does not create or download an MP3 file.

Which languages does the matching Voice Typing tool offer?

The same ToolBari view includes Voice Typing with three language choices: Bengali (Bangladesh), English (US) and English (UK). It works best in Chrome and needs microphone permission.
