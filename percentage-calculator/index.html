The three formulas

  • X% of Y = Y × X ÷ 100
  • X is what % of Y = X ÷ Y × 100
  • Change from X to Y = (Y − X) ÷ X × 100

Worked examples

  1. 15% of 2,400: 2,400 × 15 = 36,000 → 36,000 ÷ 100 = 360.
  2. 450 is what percent of 1,800? 450 ÷ 1,800 = 0.25 → × 100 = 25%.
  3. A price rises from 800 to 1,000: (1,000 − 800) ÷ 800 = 0.25 → × 100 = 25% increase.

Questions people ask

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage, then divide by 100. For example, 15% of 2,400 = 2,400 × 15 ÷ 100 = 360.

How do I find what percent one number is of another?

Divide the part by the whole, then multiply by 100. For example, 450 out of 1,800 = 450 ÷ 1,800 × 100 = 25%.

How is percentage increase or decrease calculated?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. A rise from 800 to 1,000 is (1,000 − 800) ÷ 800 × 100 = 25% increase.
