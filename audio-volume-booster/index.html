How volume boosting works

Digital audio is a long list of numbers, one per tiny slice of sound, and each number says how far the speaker should move at that moment. Boosting multiplies every number by the gain you choose: at 1.5x, a quiet voice becomes half as loud again, and at 3x it becomes three times as loud.

There is a ceiling. Numbers cannot go past the loudest possible value, so anything that would exceed it is capped — that flattening is what makes over-boosted audio sound harsh and crackly. The peak levels above show how close your file is to that ceiling before and after boosting.

When boosting helps, and when it does not

Boosting helps recordings that were made too quietly: a voice memo held far from the speaker, a lecture recorded from the back of the room, or an old clip with a low volume. If the original is clean, a modest boost makes it comfortable to listen to.

It does not help a recording that is already loud, because there is no headroom left — the loud parts only distort. It also cannot separate speech from noise: background hiss, hum and room echo get boosted by exactly the same amount as the voice.

Getting the best result

Start low and listen before going louder. A gain of 1.5x is often enough for a quiet voice memo, and you can always run the file through again if it is still too quiet. Compare the original and boosted versions with the play buttons before you download.

If the boosted version crackles on loud words, the gain is too high — drop it a notch and try again. The peak levels above the buttons show how close you are to the ceiling, so you can find the loudest clean setting for each file.

Questions people ask

Does this tool upload my audio?

No. Your file is decoded and processed inside your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

What audio formats can I upload?

Any format your browser can play, such as MP3 or WAV. The download is always a WAV file.

Why does the boosted audio sound noisy?

Gain raises everything equally, including background hiss and room noise. A lower gain keeps the noise down.

Can I make audio quieter instead?

Yes. Set the gain below 1x, for example 0.5x, and the downloaded file will be quieter than the original.
