Dead Pixel Test

This test fills your screen with solid colors and gradients so dead or stuck pixels stand out against a plain background. Click or use the arrow keys to cycle through the colors, and press Esc to leave the test.

How to run the test

Start with black. A dead pixel shows as a tiny dark dot on bright colors, and a stuck pixel shows as a tiny bright or colored dot on black. Work through white, red, green and blue in turn, looking at the whole screen each time, including the corners and edges where a faulty pixel is easy to miss.

The two gradients help with subtler problems. The grey gradient runs from black to white and can reveal uneven brightness or banding, while the color gradient runs through the rainbow and can reveal a pixel stuck on one color. Take your time: a single pixel is small, and a quick glance is not enough.

Dead pixels, stuck pixels and dust

A dead pixel stays dark on every color because it no longer lights up at all. A stuck pixel stays lit in one color, often red, green or blue, and shows most clearly against black. A hot pixel stays white on every color. Before you conclude a pixel is faulty, wipe the screen gently: dust and smudges look very much like dead pixels, and a mark that moves when you wipe it was never a pixel at all.

This test only helps you see faulty pixels; it cannot fix them. Some stuck pixels recover on their own, and some screens have a warranty policy for dead pixels, so note the color and the position of anything you find and check the policy for your device.

Questions people ask

How long should I look at each color?

Take at least a few seconds per color and scan the whole screen, including the corners. A single faulty pixel is small and easy to miss in a quick glance.

What do the different colors show?

Dark dots on bright colors are usually dead pixels. Bright or colored dots on black are usually stuck pixels. Gradients reveal uneven brightness and banding.

Can this test fix a dead pixel?

No. The test only helps you find faulty pixels by showing plain colors. It cannot repair a pixel that no longer lights up.

Does the test work on a phone?

Yes. Pick a color, tap the screen to cycle to the next one, and use the Exit button to leave the test.
