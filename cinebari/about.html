About CineBari

CineBari is a free entertainment home built for everyone — especially viewers who want things simple. One tap on Movies opens a world of movies and web series; one tap on Live TV starts a live channel instantly.

What you get

  • Movies & series in Bangla, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, Hindi, Chinese, Japanese, Pakistani and English collections
  • Dubbed collections, Anime Hub, Kids Zone, Horror, Action and every other genre
  • 173+ live TV channels — news, sports, Islamic, Kolkata Bangla, music, documentary and more
  • 12 fast servers for every title, with season & episode picker for series
  • Continue Watching and My List, saved on your own device

Our promise

No sign-up. No pop-ups. No fake buttons. Ads, where they appear, stay out of the video — watching is never disturbed.
