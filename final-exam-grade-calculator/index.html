Final Exam Grade Calculator

Enter your current grade, the weight of the final exam and the grade you want, and this calculator shows the exact score you need on the final. It also tells you whether that score is possible, so you can plan your revision with a clear target in mind.

This calculator works with the numbers you enter. It cannot know your teacher's rounding rules, grade boundaries or extra-credit policy, so check the result against your course syllabus before you rely on it.

How the calculation works

Your final grade is a weighted mix of two parts: the grade you already have and the grade you get on the final exam. If the exam is worth 30 percent, your current grade counts for the other 70 percent. The calculator first works out how many points your current grade already contributes, then finds how many points are still missing to reach your target, and finally divides those missing points by the exam weight.

As a worked example, suppose your current grade is 80, the final exam is worth 30 percent and your target is 85. Your current grade contributes 80 times 0.70, which is 56 points. You still need 85 minus 56, which is 29 points, and 29 divided by 0.30 is 96.67. So you need 96.67 percent on the final exam.

What if the score you need is over 100?

A needed score above 100 means the target cannot be reached, even with a perfect exam. The calculator tells you the best final grade you can still get, so you can set a realistic target instead. A needed score below zero means the opposite: you have already secured your target, and even a zero on the final would not drop you below it.

Use the result as a planning number. If the needed score is close to your usual exam performance, a focused revision plan may be enough. If it is far above anything you have scored before, talk to your teacher about what is realistic and whether any other graded work is still open.

Questions people ask

What if my final exam counts for half my grade?

Enter 50 as the exam weight. The calculator treats the exam as half of your final grade and your current grade as the other half.

What does a needed score below zero mean?

It means you have already reached your target grade. Even a score of zero on the final exam would leave you at or above the target you entered.

Can I use letter grades with this calculator?

No, the calculator needs numbers. Convert your letter grades to percentages first, using the scale your school or college uses, then enter those numbers.

Does the calculator round the result?

The needed score is shown to two decimal places. Your teacher may round differently, so treat a result like 89.96 as effectively 90 when you plan.
