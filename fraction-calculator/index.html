Fraction Calculator

Enter two fractions and pick add, subtract, multiply or divide to see the answer simplified to its lowest terms. The working is shown step by step, with the decimal value of the result beside it.

A denominator of zero is not allowed, because division by zero has no answer, and the calculator will say so instead of guessing. Very large numerators and denominators are rounded in the decimal display, but the fraction answer stays exact.

How the working goes

To add or subtract, both fractions first get the same denominator. Each numerator is scaled to match, then the numerators are added or subtracted while the denominator stays the same. For example, 1/2 plus 1/3 becomes 3/6 plus 2/6, which is 5/6. To multiply, multiply the numerators together and the denominators together: 3/4 times 2/3 is 6/12.

To divide, turn the second fraction upside down and multiply instead: 1/2 divided by 1/4 becomes 1/2 times 4/1, which is 4/2. Every answer is then simplified by dividing the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor, so 6/12 becomes 1/2 and 4/2 becomes 2. The calculator shows each of these steps.

Reading the answer

The answer is shown as a fraction in its lowest terms, with its decimal value beside it. When the numerator is larger than the denominator, the mixed number is shown too, so 5/3 also appears as 1 2/3. A whole number answer is shown without a denominator.

Negative fractions work as well: type a minus sign in front of a numerator. The sign stays with the numerator in the answer, and the working shows each step. A denominator of zero is not allowed, and neither is dividing by a fraction worth zero. Whole number answers are shown without a denominator.

Questions people ask

How do you add fractions with different denominators?

First give both fractions the same denominator by multiplying each one top and bottom, then add the numerators and keep the denominator. For example, 1/2 plus 1/3 becomes 3/6 plus 2/6, which is 5/6.

How do you divide one fraction by another?

Turn the second fraction upside down and multiply instead. For example, 1/2 divided by 1/4 becomes 1/2 times 4/1, which is 4/2, simplified to 2.

What does simplified to lowest terms mean?

It means the numerator and denominator share no common factor larger than 1. The calculator divides both by their greatest common divisor, so 4/6 becomes 2/3.

Can I use negative fractions?

Yes. Type a minus sign in front of the numerator for a negative fraction. The sign stays with the numerator in the answer, and the working shows each step.
