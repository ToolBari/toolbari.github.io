About ToolBari

ToolBari is a free all-in-one tools website with 432 everyday tools in one place. Most tools run directly in your browser, with no sign-up and no installation, and your files are not uploaded anywhere.

This page explains what ToolBari is, how its tools work, and how new tools get added. If you are looking for a particular tool, you can find it from the home page — everything here is free to use.

What you will find here

ToolBari brings 432 everyday tools together in one place. You will find a video downloader, image to PDF, passport-size photos, a QR code generator, a Bangla date converter, a bKash cash-out calculator, a currency converter, a CV builder, an invoice generator, prayer times, weather, government service links and much more. The tools cover the small jobs of daily life: documents and images, money and dates, study and work, and the everyday needs of users in Bangladesh — from prayer times to government services. Instead of searching for a different website for every small task, you can do them all here.

How the tools work

Most tools on ToolBari run directly in your browser. You open the tool and use it straight away — there is no account to create and nothing to install. Your files are not uploaded anywhere: images, documents and text stay on your own device while the tool works on them. Because nothing is installed, nothing takes up space on your phone and there is nothing to update — you always get the current version simply by opening the page.

Built for phones

The site is built mobile-first, because most of our visitors use phones. Every tool is designed to work well on a small screen, with buttons big enough to tap and pages that stay simple and readable. A task that takes a minute on a computer should take a minute on a phone too, and that is how the site is built. If a page feels hard to use on your phone, that is worth reporting too — the site is meant to work for the device in your hand.

Our goal

Our goal is to deliver simple, fast and completely free tools to users in Bangladesh and around the world. A small task should never require installing an app or spending money. Everything on the site follows that goal: tools are kept simple enough to use without instructions, fast enough to finish a small job in a minute, and free for everyone.

How new tools get chosen

New tools get chosen by what visitors ask for and what everyday tasks need. When people write in asking for a tool, or when a common task keeps coming up, that is what gets built next. Before a new tool is listed, it is tested in a real phone browser, so the tools you find here work on the device you actually use.

Request a new tool

Want a tool that is not here yet? On the Contact page let us know — new tools are added based on demand. Tell us what the tool should do and what you would use it for. You can also write in about a tool that is not working the way it should — reports like that help keep the tools in good shape.

Questions people ask

Is ToolBari really free?

Yes. Every tool on ToolBari is free to use, with no sign-up and no payment. A small task should never require installing an app or spending money.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use every tool without an account, and no account is created. Just open the tool and use it.

Do my files leave my device?

No. Most tools run directly in your browser, so the images, documents and text you use with them are never uploaded anywhere. Your files stay on your own device.
