UTM Builder

Short answer: paste your main link, add the source, medium and campaign, and press Create Link — the tagged tracking link is ready to copy and share.

Open the UTM Link Builder on ToolBari

The UTM builder makes tracking links by adding small tags to a web link, so you can tell whether your visitors came from a post, an email, or a campaign. It is for anyone who shares links and wants to know which ones bring people to their site. Paste in your link, add the tags, and copy the finished link. The tool works in the browser with no sign-up, so there is nothing to install. It only builds the link. It does not track visitors or show you results.

Example link

Main link https://toolbari.github.io/ with source facebook, medium social and campaign launch becomes:

https://toolbari.github.io/?utm_source=facebook&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch

What each field means

  • Main link — the page the visitor should land on.
  • utm_source — where the visit comes from, for example facebook.
  • utm_medium — the channel, for example social or email.
  • utm_campaign — the name of the post or campaign, for example launch.
  • utm_term (optional) — the paid keyword, when the link runs in an ad.
  • utm_content (optional) — tells two links in the same campaign apart.

Questions people ask

What are UTM parameters?

UTM parameters are short tags added to the end of a link — utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign — that tell analytics tools where a visitor came from, so you can see which post, page or campaign brought the visit.

Do UTM parameters change the page a visitor sees?

No. The tags only carry tracking information; the visitor lands on the same page as the plain link.

Which UTM fields are required?

In this builder you fill in the main link, utm_source, utm_medium and utm_campaign. utm_term and utm_content are optional extras for paid keywords and for telling two links in the same campaign apart.

Is the UTM Builder free?

Yes — free, with no sign-up, and it runs in your browser on any phone or computer.

Does the tool show me who clicked my link?

No. It only builds the link with the tags on it. To see the results, you look at your own website's visitor reports.
